# 26 — Player profiles, psychology, motivation (2026-08-22)

Validity-graded empirical foundations for the design module.

## The validity ladder

- Motivations are CONTINUOUS, INDEPENDENT DIMENSIONS — never exclusive
  types (Yee 2006 refuted Bartle's suppression assumption; every
  psychometric audit since agrees: traits replicate, discrete types
  don't). Model players as motivation vectors; discrete labels are
  presentation sugar only.
- **Quantic Foundry GMM** = strongest industry model: 12 motivations in 6
  pairs / 3 clusters (Action-Social, Mastery-Achievement,
  Immersion-Creativity); citable N = 466k (v2.3, 2023-2025). Bartle =
  historical vocabulary only (no statistical validation). BrainHex
  deprecated (α .68-.78; 6-month stability .40-.70). **Trojan Player
  Typology (2015) = the behaviorally validated one**: 6 motivations
  validated against server telemetry, N=18.6k LoL + 18.8k Chinese MMO.
- **SDT/PENS**: competence/autonomy/relatedness satisfaction predicts
  enjoyment and continued play; levers: masterable controls, clear
  feedback, goal/strategy choice, opt-in co-op. PENS is PROPRIETARY;
  **BANGS (2024, open access, measures need frustration too) is the
  modern open instrument.**

## Findings worth encoding (each with source+year in the table)

Competition declines most with age (steepest 13→35; age explains >2×
gender's variance — "the elephant is age, not gender"); Strategy most
age-stable; female share by genre spans Match-3 69% ↔ tactical shooter
4%; shooters trade Competition↔Story; strategy audiences cross a
"cognitive threshold"; QF Dec 2025: players overwhelmingly negative on
gen-AI, varying by profile. Big-Five bridge (E↔Action-Social,
C↔Mastery-Achievement, O↔Immersion-Creativity) is a coarse prior only
(r≈.1-.3).

Telemetry-scale: motivational QUALITY beats playtime for well-being
(Vuorre 2022, N=38,935, 7 publishers); social ties = strongest
multiplayer retention mechanism; retention drivers shift
achievement-early → social-late; purchases predicted by unobstructed
play + social interaction + economical rationale, not generic engagement
(Hamari 2017).

Demographics (dated, source-attributed): ESA 2025 — 205M US players,
avg age 36, 52/47 M/F adults, ~21% report a disability; Newzoo 2025 —
$188.8B, 3.6B players, 44% payers. Spender tiers encoded as RANGES
(whales top 1-5% ≈ 50-70% IAP revenue).

# 29 — Game market trends 2024-2026 (2026-08-22)

Every number carries year+source in the module tables; estimates labeled
"estimated"; stat-aggregator content farms excluded by rule.

## Shape

2025 global: $188.8B (+3.4%), mobile $103B / console $45.9B / PC $39.9B
(Newzoo); 3.578B players, 44% payers at $119.70/yr. B2P playtime growing
on all platforms while F2P playtime declines (Fortnite −29% YoY, CoD
−33%, 2025); console microtransactions were the only declining
sub-segment (−6.3%). Subscriptions = biggest US growth driver of 2025
(Circana). Attention economics: new releases capture only 12-13% of
PC/console playtime (stable 4 years); ~60% goes to games 6+ years old;
Roblox = 9.7% of ALL PC playtime (+52%, 2025).

## Steam reality

2025: ~20k releases (+11%, record); only ~8% earned >$100k; 40% didn't
recoup the $100 fee; median release ~$249 gross; top 1% of games ≈ 84.5%
of revenue (estimates). Counterpoint (Zukowski): 2.99% of 2025 releases
hit 1,000+ reviews (~$150k proxy) — the HIGHEST success rate on record;
a real indie golden age atop a giant zero-revenue tail. Wishlists:
first-week ≈ 0.15× median; Next Fest amplifies existing momentum
(68-88% of wishlists come from people who never played the demo). Early
Access: the 1.0 bump collapsed to ~40% of EA-launch revenue — **EA
launch IS the launch**.

## Genre winners/losers (2024-2026, data-backed)

Winners: extraction shooters (ARC Raiders 16.3M by Q2 2026); **3D co-op
"friendslop" at $8-25** (R.E.P.O. ~18M, Peak ~16M, Schedule I ~8M
estimated — eight new indies passed 100k CCU in 2025); survival-craft
(20.8% hit rate per release — the best), farming 8.3%, roguelike
deckbuilder 5.1%; horror (#3 by count); narrative (#1 by count);
AA at $40-50 validated (Clair Obscur: <$10M budget, ~30-person core,
8M+ units). Losers: battle royale playtime −27%; mobile RPG −14.7%;
new AAA F2P (Concord dead in 2 weeks; Marvel Rivals the sole newer F2P
in 2025's platinum tier); annualized sports playtime declining.
Live-service contraction: XDefiant, MultiVersus, Dauntless, King of Meat
shut; Sony cancelled 8 of 12 planned live-service titles. Remakes:
$1.4B from 72.4M players (Jan 2024-Sep 2025); remakes earn 2.2× remasters.

## Platforms & policy

Roblox DevEx >$1.5B (2025), top-1,000 creators avg $1.3M — steep power
law; Fortnite Creator Economy $352M (2024), engagement-based. Epic v.
Apple (Apr 2025): zero-commission external purchase links in the US; EU
DMA fine €500M. Cross-play is table stakes. Handheld: Steam Deck ~5M
lifetime (estimated), Deck-Verified is now a standard checklist item;
Switch 2 fastest Nintendo launch ever (17.37M by Dec 2025, verified).
GTA VI Nov 19, 2026 = the scheduling tent-pole. Prices: $80 tier opened
and met resistance (Xbox reversed); sub-$30 PC segment +40% since 2022;
small-team sweet spot $10-25.

## AI in games

~20% of 2025 Steam releases disclosed gen-AI (7× growth); Valve
disclosure required since Jan 2024 (Jan 2026 rewrite: player-facing
content only); itch.io tagging mandatory. Sentiment-behavior gap:
QF Dec 2025 = 85% negative; Circana = only ~25% say AI reduces purchase
likelihood. Backlash concentrates on VISIBLE sloppy assets and voice
replication (ARC Raiders re-recorded lines — and still sold 16M+).
SAG-AFTRA 2025 agreement: digital replicas need informed consent +
disclosure + rates. Module defaults: disclose, keep AI invisible-infra
or clearly stylized, never voice/portrait without consent.

## Evidence-backed opportunity map (small team / agent-built 3D)

1. 3D co-op $8-25, physics/proximity-voice emergent comedy, streamable
   moments, stylized not photoreal, EA-friendly — the best-evidenced
   niche of 2024-2026.
2. Survival-craft / sim / management — highest per-release hit rates.
3. Horror — best count-volume for small 3D teams.
4. Avoid: platformers (≤0.18% hit rate), pure puzzle (0.34%), new F2P
   live-service (graveyard), competing with the 60% back-catalog gravity
   well beyond the first two weeks.
5. Plan Early Access as the marketing apex; bring ≥7-10k wishlists to
   Next Fest; premium co-op with light post-launch content (Helldivers 2
   / ARC Raiders model) over F2P.

## Reporting inconsistency worth a line in the protocol

At the same instant, the same lane reports two different values for the same
field name:

- `tee_status.adapters.fusion.connected` = **false**
- `fu_probe.connected` = **true**

Neither is wrong. They answer different questions — "is there an active design
to act on" versus "is the bridge reachable" — but they share a field name, so a
reader comparing two receipts can conclude a lane is flapping when nothing
changed. §4E already says an offline DCC is not by itself a failure; the
sharper rule this case wants is that **a lane's `connected` must be read with
the probe that produced it**, and a receipt should say which. Offering it for
the next revision rather than asserting it.

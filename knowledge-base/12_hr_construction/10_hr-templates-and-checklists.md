# HR templates and checklists for construction

> ⚠️ **THESE ARE TEMPLATES, NOT LEGAL DOCUMENTS.** Every document below is a skeleton written to illustrate structure and required content. It has **not** been drafted or reviewed by a legal practitioner in Namibia or South Africa, it does not account for any applicable bargaining council agreement, sectoral determination or company-specific policy, and figures shown are placeholders. **Have each one reviewed by a labour lawyer or registered labour consultant in the relevant jurisdiction before you use it with a real employee.** Using an unreviewed template is how small firms lose CCMA and Labour Commissioner cases.

**Summary.** Ten reusable documents that cover the construction employment lifecycle: contract, job description, induction, leave, time, discipline, performance, exit, and the personnel file index that holds them all. Fields marked `[ ]` are to be completed; fields marked `«…»` are drafting choices. Content is aligned to **[ZA]** BCEA ss.29, 31, 33, 42 and LRA Schedule 8, and **[NA]** Labour Act 11 of 2007 ss.10–12, 16–26, 30, 33, 35, 37.

## 1. Employment contract skeleton

**CONTRACT OF EMPLOYMENT — «PERMANENT / FIXED-TERM / PROJECT-BASED»**

Entered into between **[Employer full legal name]**, registration number `[ ]`, of `[registered address]` ("the Employer") and **[Employee full names]**, ID/passport number `[ ]`, of `[address]` ("the Employee").

1. **Commencement.** Employment commences on `[date]`.
2. **Type and duration.** *«Permanent, subject to termination in accordance with clause 12.»* / *«Fixed term ending on [date].»* / *«Project-based, terminating automatically on the occurrence of the following event: completion of [defined works] at [project name and erf/stand number]. The Employee acknowledges that no expectation of further employment arises.»*
3. **Probation.** `[ ]` months, during which performance will be assessed against clause 5 and the Employee will receive feedback and an opportunity to improve.
4. **Position.** `[Job title]`, reporting to `[position]`.
5. **Duties.** As set out in the attached Job Description (Annexure A), and such other reasonable duties within the Employee's competence as may be assigned.
6. **Place of work.** `[Site]`. The Employer may require the Employee to work at any of its sites within `[defined area]`, on notice, with «transport provided / a travel allowance of [ ] per day».
7. **Hours.** Ordinary hours are `[ ]` per week, `[ ]` per day, worked `[days]` from `[start]` to `[finish]`, with a meal interval of `[ ]`. Ordinary hours may not exceed 45 per week.
8. **Overtime.** Overtime is worked by agreement and is limited to 10 hours per week. Overtime is paid at 1.5 × the basic hourly rate; Sunday work at 2 × «or 1.5 × if the Employee ordinarily works Sundays **[ZA]**»; public holiday work per «s.18 BCEA **[ZA]**» / «s.22 Labour Act **[NA]**». **[NA]** night work between 20h00 and 07h00 attracts an additional 6 %.
9. **Remuneration.** `[N$/R]` `[ ]` per «hour / day / week / month», payable «weekly / fortnightly / monthly» in arrears on `[pay day]` by «electronic transfer / mobile money / cash against signature».
10. **Allowances.** `[travel / subsistence / accommodation / height / tool]` — `[amount and conditions]`.
11. **Deductions.** The Employer will deduct: «PAYE; social security **[NA]** / UIF **[ZA]**; bargaining council contributions **[ZA]**»; and any amount the Employee authorises in writing. Total deductions will not exceed one third of remuneration **[NA]** s.12. Deductions for loss or damage will only be made where the loss occurred in the course of employment through the Employee's fault, after the Employee has had a fair opportunity to respond, and will not exceed the actual loss or one quarter of remuneration **[ZA]** s.34 BCEA.
12. **Leave.** Annual leave: «21 consecutive days **[ZA]** / 4 consecutive weeks **[NA]**» per cycle, taken at times agreed with the Employer having regard to the programme. Sick leave: per «s.22 BCEA» / «s.24 Labour Act». «Family responsibility leave: 3 days **[ZA]**» / «Compassionate leave: 5 working days **[NA]**». Maternity/parental leave per statute.
13. **Notice of termination.** «1 week / 2 weeks / 4 weeks **[ZA]** s.37» or «1 day / 1 week / 1 month **[NA]** s.30» according to length of service, in writing by either party.
14. **Health and safety.** The Employee will comply with the site health and safety plan, wear all PPE issued at the Employer's cost, report every injury, incident and near miss immediately, and submit to «medical fitness examination **[ZA]** Annexure 3» and to alcohol and drug testing in accordance with the Employer's policy.
15. **Company property.** Tools and equipment issued are recorded in the Tool Issue Register and remain the Employer's property, to be returned on termination.
16. **Confidentiality.** The Employee will not disclose the Employer's pricing, client information or methods.
17. **Disciplinary and grievance procedures.** The Employer's Disciplinary Code and Grievance Procedure (Annexure B) form part of this contract; the Employee acknowledges receiving a copy and having it explained.
18. **Whole agreement and variation.** This is the whole agreement; no variation is effective unless in writing and signed.

Signed at `[place]` on `[date]`. Employer: `[name, capacity, signature]`. Employee: `[signature]`. Witness: `[name, signature]`.
*Explained to the Employee in `[language]` by `[name]`.*

## 11. Site personnel file index

One file per employee, in this order, with a contents page at the front. Medical and disciplinary records in separately secured sections.

| Tab | Contents |
|---|---|
| **A — Identity** | Application form; CV; ID/passport copy; work permit; proof of address; next of kin and emergency contact |
| **B — Engagement** | Signed contract; job description; offer letter; probation confirmation; any variation letters |
| **C — Credentials** | Trade and plant certificates with **verification references and dates**; driving licence and PrDP; professional registrations; qualification verification correspondence |
| **D — Induction and training** | Induction checklist; toolbox talk attendance record; training certificates; competency matrix extract; learnership/apprenticeship logbook |
| **E — Appointments** | Written statutory appointments and acceptances; scope; competence evidence; expiry dates |
| **F — Payroll** | Banking/mobile money details; tax registration; «social security / UIF» registration; payslip copies; deduction authorisations; garnishee or maintenance orders |
| **G — Time and leave** | Timesheet copies or payroll extracts; leave record; leave application forms; medical certificates for sick leave |
| **H — PPE and tools** | PPE issue register entries; tool issue register |
| **I — Performance** | Performance reviews; performance improvement plans; counselling records; commendations |
| **J — Discipline** *(restricted)* | Warnings with validity dates; hearing notices; minutes; outcome letters; appeal records; grievances lodged |
| **K — Medical** *(restricted, separate storage)* | Medical certificate of fitness **[ZA]** Annexure 3; occupational health surveillance results (outcome only); injury on duty reports and claim forms; return-to-work assessments |
| **L — Exit** | Resignation/termination letter; exit checklist; final settlement calculation; certificate of service; exit interview notes |

**Retention.** **[ZA]** BCEA s.31 requires records to be kept for **three years** from the date of the last entry. Keep injury, medical surveillance and compensation records **much longer** — occupational disease claims surface decades later. **Access control:** tabs J and K on a need-to-know basis only. **[ZA]** POPIA treats health and biometric information as special personal information; process it lawfully and store it separately.

## Open questions

- These templates have **not** been reviewed by a legal practitioner in either jurisdiction — that review is a prerequisite to use.
- No bargaining council main agreement wording has been incorporated; **[ZA]** employers in a council's registered scope must align contract, wage, leave and disciplinary provisions to the extended agreement.
- **[NA]** record retention periods under the Labour Act were not verified; the three-year period stated is the **[ZA]** BCEA s.31 period applied as good practice.
- The warning validity periods (3/6/12 months) and the appeal timeframes are common practice, not statutory.

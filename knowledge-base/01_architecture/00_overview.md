# Architecture domain overview

**Summary.** This domain covers everything required to become — and then to work as — an architect, with a primary focus on Namibia **[NA]** and South Africa **[ZA]**, and RIBA/ARB (UK) and NCARB (US) treated as international reference models. It runs from the statutory route to registration, through accredited degree programmes and the model curriculum, into the four working competences an architect is actually paid for: design judgement, drawn documentation, building science, and structural literacy — and closes with professional practice (fees, contracts, liability) and a curated reading list. It is written for machine retrieval: hard numbers, clause references, statute names and tables first; prose second.

## Key facts

| Fact | **[ZA]** South Africa | **[NA]** Namibia |
|---|---|---|
| Statute | Architectural Profession Act 44 of 2000 | Architects' and Quantity Surveyors' Act 13 of 1979 |
| Regulator | South African Council for the Architectural Profession (SACAP) | Namibia Council for Architects and Quantity Surveyors (NCAQS) |
| Umbrella / built-environment body | Council for the Built Environment (CBE), CBE Act 43 of 2000 | — (council reports to the responsible Minister) |
| Voluntary association | South African Institute of Architects (SAIA) + regional institutes | Namibia Institute of Architects (NIA) |
| Registration categories | 4 professional + 4 candidate (Act s.18) | Architect, Quantity Surveyor, Architect in Training, Quantity Surveyor in Training (Act s.11) |
| Accredited schools | 11 Architectural Learning Sites | NUST (BArch, BArch Hons, MArch) |
| Terminal professional degree | MArch (Professional), NQF 9 | Master of Architecture (09MARC), NQF 9 |
| Post-degree candidacy | ≥ 2 years (architect/technologist); ≥ 3 years (draughtsperson) | Prescribed period of practical training under Act s.11(2) — **needs verification** for exact length |
| Final examination | SACAP Professional Practice Examination (PPE), 2 papers × 3 h | Prescribed examination under Act s.11(2)(b) — form **needs verification** |
| Typical total time, school leaver → registered architect | 8–10 years | 8–10 years |

> ⚠️ In both jurisdictions, performing reserved architectural work for gain while unregistered is a statutory offence — **[ZA]** Architectural Profession Act s.41; **[NA]** Architects' and Quantity Surveyors' Act s.13. Registration is not optional professional decoration.

## What the domain covers

Architecture is four overlapping bodies of knowledge plus one licence:

1. **Design** — the generative craft. Parti, site, brief, circulation, proportion, sequence, light, materiality, tectonics. This is the part that cannot be looked up, but it *does* have rules of thumb and dimensional discipline (file `03`).
2. **Documentation** — the legal and instructional instrument. Drawings, schedules, specifications, revision control. A building is procured and defended from the drawing set, not from the idea (file `04`).
3. **Building science** — thermal, moisture, ventilation, daylight, acoustics, solar geometry. In Namibia's hot semi-arid and hot steppe climates this is the difference between a habitable and an uninhabitable building (file `05`).
4. **Structural literacy** — enough load-path reasoning and preliminary sizing to design a buildable form and to hold a credible conversation with an engineer, without pretending to be one (file `06`).
5. **Practice** — services stages, fee calculation, appointment agreements, contract administration, certificates, insurance, ethics and legal exposure (file `07`).

## Learning sequence

A pragmatic order of study, whether formally enrolled or self-directing:

**Phase 1 — Orientation (weeks 1–4).** Read `00`, then `01`. Decide the target registration category *before* choosing an institution; **[ZA]** SACAP explicitly warns that the category determines which qualification is useful. Skim `08` and acquire two books: a dimensional reference (Neufert or Metric Handbook) and Ching's *Architecture: Form, Space, and Order*.

**Phase 2 — Representation (months 1–6).** File `04` before file `03`. Learning to draw plan, section and elevation at correct scale with correct line weights is the prerequisite for design conversation. Draw existing buildings measured from life; redraw published buildings at 1:100 and 1:50.

**Phase 3 — Design fundamentals (months 3–18, continuous).** File `03`. Run in parallel with representation. Design studio is iterative and cannot be front-loaded; the dimensional rules of thumb in `03` should be memorised, not consulted.

**Phase 4 — Technical substrate (months 6–30).** Files `05` and `06`, alongside the construction technology material in domain `02_building_construction`. Work a real climate: compute solar altitudes for your latitude, size an overhang, calculate a wall U-value. See `05` for worked Windhoek (22.56° S) and Ohangwena (~17.5° S) examples.

**Phase 5 — Codes (months 12 onward).** Domain `03_codes_standards`. **[ZA]** SANS 10400 parts A–XA. **[NA]** local-authority building regulations (e.g. Municipality of Windhoek Building Regulations, G.N. 57 of 1969) plus Namibian-adopted standards.

**Phase 6 — Practice (final year of study, and throughout candidacy).** File `07`. This is what the **[ZA]** PPE examines and what candidacy logbooks record.

**Phase 7 — Candidacy and examination.** Register as a candidate immediately on graduating; the clock only starts once you are registered. **[ZA]** minimum two years and 24 Monthly Training Records for candidate architects/technologists.

## Conventions used in this domain

- Dimensions in millimetres for construction, metres for planning, unless stated.
- U-values in W/m²·K; R-values in m²·K/W. R = 1/U for an assembly.
- Loads in kPa (= kN/m²).
- Currency shown as `R` (rand) or `N$` (Namibian dollar) with the date of the quote. The Namibian dollar is pegged 1:1 to the rand.
- Statute and standard references are given with clause numbers where verified; where a number could not be confirmed from a primary source, the file is marked `needs-verification` and the gap is stated in `## Open questions`.

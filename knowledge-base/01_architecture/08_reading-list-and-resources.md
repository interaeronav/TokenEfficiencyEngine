# Reading list, standards and open resources for architects

**Summary.** A working architect needs three distinct libraries: **reference manuals** consulted daily for dimensions and details; **technical texts** read once and re-read at need; and **theory** that shapes judgement rather than answering questions. This file separates them, states what each book is actually for, and lists standards, open-access resources, journals, podcasts and free courses. Editions turn over — verify the current edition before buying.

> ⚠️ Book editions change frequently. Every edition and ISBN below that is not explicitly marked *verified* should be re-checked with the publisher before purchase. This file is marked `needs-verification` for that reason.

## Key facts — the five books to own first

| Priority | Book | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Neufert, *Architects' Data*** (Wiley) | The dimensional bible. Look up any space type and get plans, clearances and standards |
| 2 | **Buxton (ed.), *Metric Handbook: Planning and Design Data*, 7th ed., Routledge, 2021, ISBN 9781003052586** *(verified)* | Building-type design data with a UK/metric bias; better than Neufert on briefing and area planning |
| 3 | **Ching, *Architecture: Form, Space, and Order*, 5th ed., Wiley, ISBN 9781119853374** *(verified)* | The visual grammar of design: form, organisation, circulation, proportion, ordering principles |
| 4 | **Ching, *Building Construction Illustrated*** (Wiley) | How buildings go together, drawn. The single best bridge from design to detail |
| 5 | **Allen & Iano, *Fundamentals of Building Construction: Materials and Methods*, 7th ed., Wiley, ISBN 9781119446194** *(verified)* | The systematic account of materials and assembly sequences |

## 1. Reference manuals (consult, don't read)

| Book | Author / editor | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| *Architects' Data* | Ernst Neufert (Wiley) | Dimensions and layouts for every building type; the default first look-up |
| *Metric Handbook: Planning and Design Data*, 7th ed. | Pamela Buxton (ed.), Routledge, 2021 | Building types, briefing, area standards, materials, acoustics, lighting, human dimensions |
| *Building Construction Illustrated* | Francis D. K. Ching (Wiley) | Drawn construction assemblies from foundation to roof |
| *A Visual Dictionary of Architecture* | Francis D. K. Ching (Wiley) | Terminology, drawn. Settles vocabulary arguments |
| *Building Codes Illustrated* | Francis D. K. Ching & Steven Winkel (Wiley) | US IBC, but the *reasoning* behind code provisions transfers directly to SANS 10400 |
| *Architectural Graphic Standards* | AIA / Wiley | Exhaustive US detail reference; heavy, expensive, occasionally indispensable |
| *Time-Saver Standards for Architectural Design Data* | McGraw-Hill | Older, still useful for building-type planning |
| *Handbook of Building Construction* / SA construction texts | see domain `02_building_construction` | Local materials, trades and practice |

## 2. Technical texts (read once, keep)

**Construction and materials**
- Deplazes (ed.), *Constructing Architecture: Materials, Processes, Structures* (Birkhäuser) — the best single account of tectonics as a design discipline.
- Ford, *The Details of Modern Architecture*, vols 1–2 (MIT Press) — how canonical modern buildings were actually detailed, and where they failed.
- Schittich et al., *Detail Construction Manual* series (Birkhäuser/Detail): Glass, Facade, Timber, Concrete, Masonry, Roof, Flat Roof, Refurbishment. Expensive; the Facade and Glass volumes are the most useful for domain `08_glass_and_facades`.
- Emmitt & Gorse, *Barry's Introduction to Construction of Buildings* and *Barry's Advanced Construction of Buildings* (Wiley) — the standard UK teaching texts.

**Structures**
- Ching, Onouye & Zuberbuhler, *Building Structures Illustrated* (Wiley) — structures explained through architectural drawing.
- Allen & Zalewski, *Form and Forces: Designing Efficient, Expressive Structures* (Wiley) — graphic statics; makes structural intuition teachable.
- Salvadori, *Why Buildings Stand Up* (Norton) — the readable introduction; pair with *Why Buildings Fall Down* (Levy & Salvadori).
- Schodek & Bechthold, *Structures* (Pearson) — the more rigorous textbook.

**Environment and building science**
- Lechner & Andrasik, *Heating, Cooling, Lighting: Sustainable Design Strategies Towards Net Zero Architecture*, 5th ed., Wiley, ISBN 9781119585749 *(verified)* — the core environmental design text for architects.
- Brown & DeKay, *Sun, Wind, and Light: Architectural Design Strategies*, 3rd ed., Wiley, ISBN 9780470945780 *(verified)* — strategies organised as design moves with sizing rules. The most directly usable book on this list for Namibian conditions.
- Olgyay, *Design with Climate: Bioclimatic Approach to Architectural Regionalism* (Princeton) — the founding text; the bioclimatic chart still works.
- Givoni, *Man, Climate and Architecture* / *Passive and Low Energy Cooling of Buildings* — the technical backbone for hot-dry design.
- Fathy, *Natural Energy and Vernacular Architecture* (University of Chicago) — hot-arid passive strategies from the Middle East and North Africa, directly transferable to Namibia.
- Egan, *Architectural Acoustics* (J. Ross) — the standard architectural acoustics primer.

**Practice**
- AIA, *The Architect's Handbook of Professional Practice* (Wiley) — the US reference; principles transfer, procedure does not. 15th edition ISBN 9781118308820; check for a later edition.
- Ostime, *RIBA Job Book* and *RIBA Plan of Work 2020 Guide* series (RIBA Publishing) — the best procedural model available in English.
- Local practice texts: see domain `04_masters_and_practice` for **[ZA]/[NA]** JBCC and PROCSA guidance.

**Representation**
- Ching, *Architectural Graphics* (Wiley) — the fundamentals of drawing, still unbeaten.
- Ching, *Design Drawing* (Wiley) — freehand, perspective and sketching as thinking.
- Farrelly, *Representational Techniques* / *Construction and Materiality* (Basics Architecture series, Bloomsbury) — cheap, useful student primers.

## 4. Standards and codes

**[ZA] South Africa**

| Standard | Subject |
|---|---|
| SANS 10400 (Parts A–XA) | Application of the National Building Regulations. Key parts: **A** general, **C** dimensions, **D** public safety, **F** site operations, **J** floors, **K** walls, **L** roofs, **M** stairways, **N** glazing, **O** lighting and ventilation, **P** drainage, **Q** non-water-borne sanitation, **R** stormwater, **S** facilities for persons with disabilities, **T** fire protection, **V** space heating, **W** fire installation, **XA** energy usage |
| SANS 10143 (SABS 0143) | Building drawing practice |
| SANS 10160 (Parts 1–8) | Basis of structural design and actions: -1 basis, **-2 imposed loads**, **-3 wind**, **-4 seismic**, -5 crane/machinery, -6 industrial, -7 thermal, -8 execution |
| SANS 10100 | Structural use of concrete |
| SANS 10162 | Structural use of steel |
| SANS 10163 | Structural use of timber |
| SANS 10164 | Structural use of masonry |
| SANS 204 | Energy efficiency in buildings |
| SANS 10252 | Water supply and drainage installations |
| SANS 10142 | Wiring of premises |

**[NA] Namibia**
- **Architects' and Quantity Surveyors' Act 13 of 1979** — the professional statute.
- **Municipality of Windhoek Building Regulations, promulgated by G.N. 57 of 28 April 1969** — Windhoek building control; other local authorities have their own.
- **Windhoek Town Planning Scheme** — zoning, coverage, bulk, building lines.
- **Namibian Standards Institution (NSI)** — publishes Namibian adopted standards; catalogue at nsi.com.na.

**International reference**
- ISO 6946 (thermal resistance and transmittance), ISO 13788 (condensation), ISO 128 (technical drawing), ISO 5457 (drawing sheet sizes), ISO 19650 (BIM information management), ISO 7730 (thermal comfort), ASHRAE 55 and 62.1, ANSI S12.60 (classroom acoustics), Eurocodes (EN 1990–1999).

## 9. Suggested acquisition order and budget

| Stage | Acquire |
|---|---|
| Year 1 | Ching *Architectural Graphics*; Ching *Form, Space, and Order*; a scale rule set, drawing board, and a good sketchbook |
| Year 2 | Ching *Building Construction Illustrated*; Neufert **or** *Metric Handbook*; Ching *Building Structures Illustrated* |
| Year 3 | Allen & Iano *Fundamentals of Building Construction*; Brown & DeKay *Sun, Wind, and Light*; access to SANS 10400 |
| Honours / Master's | Deplazes *Constructing Architecture*; Lechner *Heating, Cooling, Lighting*; Frampton *Modern Architecture*; Pallasmaa *The Eyes of the Skin* |
| Candidacy | *RIBA Job Book* or equivalent; JBCC documents; PROCSA agreements; a practice-management text |

Buy reference manuals in print (you will flip, not search) and theory in whatever format you will actually read. Second-hand previous editions of *Neufert*, *Metric Handbook* and *Architectural Graphic Standards* are perfectly serviceable at a fraction of the price — dimensional data does not change quickly.

## Open questions

- **Editions and ISBNs for the majority of titles were not individually verified.** Only Metric Handbook (7th, 2021, 9781003052586), Architecture: Form, Space, and Order (5th, 9781119853374), Fundamentals of Building Construction (7th, 9781119446194), Heating Cooling Lighting (5th, 9781119585749) and Sun Wind and Light (3rd, 9780470945780) were confirmed against publisher pages. Neufert, Ching's *Building Construction Illustrated*, *Architectural Graphic Standards* and the AIA *Handbook* are cited without a verified current edition (Wiley's pages returned 403/404 at time of access).
- *The Architect's Handbook of Professional Practice* is listed at 15th ed. (ISBN 9781118308820) from a retail listing; a 16th edition may exist.
- The podcast, journal and course lists are curated recommendations, not verified against publisher/producer pages; availability changes.
- No Namibia-specific architectural practice manual or textbook was located. If one exists (e.g. published by the NIA), it should be added.
- Whether SANS standards are available free of charge to South African users (they were at some periods for the SANS 10400 series) could not be confirmed; the archived copies linked in other files in this domain are third-party scans, not official distributions.

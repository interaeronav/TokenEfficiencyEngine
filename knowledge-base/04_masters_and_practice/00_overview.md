# Masters and practice domain overview

**Summary.** This domain records how the best architects and builders of the modern era were actually *made* — what they studied, where, under whom, what they read, how they draw, how they think, and how they run an office — and extracts from that the transferable method. It is deliberately not a celebrity file. A laureate list is only useful here because it points at a documented formation: Francis Kéré was a carpenter before he was an architect; Peter Zumthor was a cabinetmaker before he was an architect; Renzo Piano grew up in a family of builders; Tadao Ando never took a degree at all. Those are facts a person in Windhoek can act on. The domain is written for a hot-climate, resource-constrained, craft-based context, so the emphasis falls on practitioners who worked with little money, local labour and local material and still produced work of the first rank.

## The argument for studying formation rather than buildings

Photographs of finished buildings are the least instructive part of an architect's output. They show a result whose causes have been carefully erased. What is transferable is upstream of the photograph:

1. **Training is legible and repeatable.** Zumthor's apprenticeship, Doshi's four years in Le Corbusier's atelier, Moneo's three years at the Spanish Academy in Rome, Yamamoto's field trips with Hiroshi Hara across the Mediterranean and Latin America — each is a describable programme that a motivated person can approximate with local materials and a bus ticket.
2. **Constraint is the common denominator, not budget.** The practitioners in this domain who matter most for Namibia — Kéré, Doshi, Aravena, Lari, Liu Jiakun, Rich, Mpahlwa — did their defining work under *severe* material, financial and skills constraints, and treated those constraints as the generative device rather than as an excuse. File `05` documents this habit with attributed quotations.
3. **The office is a designed object too.** Murcutt runs a one-person practice and refuses large commissions. Zumthor keeps roughly fifteen people in a village in Graubünden. Aravena calls ELEMENTAL a "Do Tank" and open-sources its housing drawings. Grafton Architects named the firm after a street rather than after themselves. These are deliberate structural decisions with consequences for fee, risk and quality — covered in file `06`.
4. **Builders are excluded from the architectural literature and should not be.** The award systems that judge *delivery* — Master Builders, SAFCEC, cidb/Construction World, the FMB Master Builder Awards, AGC's Build America Awards, FIDIC and ENR — reward things architecture prizes never measure: safety record, programme certainty, defect rates, subcontractor management, and repeat client work. File `03` treats them as a parallel canon.
5. **The African record is thin in print and must be assembled deliberately.** There is no single reference work on Namibian practice. File `02` assembles what is verifiable about African and southern African practitioners and flags clearly where the record could not be confirmed.

## How to use this domain

Read `01` and `02` for the register and the biographies; then treat `04` as the curriculum and `05` as the set of mental moves to rehearse while working through it. `03` and `06` are the commercial half: they answer "and how does any of this get built and paid for". A reader in Namibia should note that four of the practitioners here worked directly in West or southern Africa — Kéré in Burkina Faso, Vassal in Niger, Adeyemi in Lagos, Rich at Mapungubwe — and that their common toolkit is earth, vault, shade, ventilation stack, local labour training and low mechanisation. That is a Namibian toolkit.

## Verification policy used in this domain

Biographical facts here were checked against the Pritzker Prize's own laureate pages, RIBA's award pages, practice websites and encyclopaedic entries with cited references. Where two sources disagree — for example Doshi's studio founding year (1955 per encyclopaedic sources, 1956 per the Pritzker biography) or Murcutt's awarding institution (University of New South Wales per the Pritzker biography, Sydney Technical College per encyclopaedic sources) — both are stated and the conflict flagged rather than silently resolved. Where nothing could be verified, the item is listed under `## Open questions` rather than filled in.

> ⚠️ Do not treat the profiles in this domain as due diligence on a living person. File `02` records that serious misconduct allegations were made against one profiled architect in 2023 and that several institutions ended engagements as a result. Reputational facts change; award records do not.

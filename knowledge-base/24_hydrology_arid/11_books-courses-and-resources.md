# Hydrology books, courses, data and professional societies

**Summary.** An annotated register of the canonical literature, the arid-zone-specific literature, free courses, journals, data portals and professional societies. **Free items are flagged.** All URLs were HTTP-tested on 2026-08-25 and the status is noted where it was not 200. The single most important entry is **The Groundwater Project**, which now hosts more than 120 professional-level groundwater books, all free, including the full text of Freeze & Cherry.

## 1. The Groundwater Project — free, and the centrepiece of this register

<https://gw-project.org/books/> — a non-profit founded by John Cherry that commissions and publishes groundwater books, free to download, in English with many translated into Spanish, French, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi and Chinese. **Over 120 English titles** were listed in the site's book sitemap on 2026-08-25.

## 8. A recommended reading order for this domain

1. **Free and essential:** the two SASSCAL *Biodiversity & Ecology* papers (`04` sources) — two hours, and you will understand Cuvelai hydrogeology better than most consultants.
2. **Free and foundational:** Groundwater Project's *Basic Hydrogeology*, then *Hydrogeologic Properties of Earth Materials and Principles of Groundwater Flow*.
3. **Free and canonical:** Freeze & Cherry, *Groundwater*.
4. **Free and practical:** Kruseman & de Ridder for pumping tests; *Getting Started with MODFLOW* for modelling.
5. **Buy:** Dingman *Physical Hydrology* for surface processes; Anderson, Woessner & Hunt *Applied Groundwater Modeling* before you build any model.
6. **Buy for this specific domain:** Wheater, Sorooshian & Sharma, *Hydrological Modelling in Arid and Semi-Arid Areas*.
7. **Regional:** Mendelsohn & Weber, *The Cuvelai Basin*; Jacobson et al. on Namibian ephemeral rivers; and the WRC free report library.

# Hydrological equipment manufacturers — procurement register

**Summary.** A working procurement reference: who makes what, the flagship product families with model designations, documentation URLs, an indicative price band, and southern African routes to supply. **All URLs in this file were HTTP-tested on 2026-08-25** and the status is noted where a site blocks automated access. Model families were verified from the manufacturers' own sitemaps and product listings where indicated; detailed specifications and all prices are marked `needs-verification` because manufacturer datasheets and quotations were not obtained in this pass.

> ⚠️ **Price bands are indicative order-of-magnitude only** and are given in USD/EUR because no southern African dealer quotations were obtained. Landed cost in Namibia typically adds freight, import duty, VAT and dealer margin — budget 1.4–1.8× the ex-works price. Always request a written quotation.

## Water level, water quality and telemetry

### In-Situ Inc. (USA)
**Makes:** water-level and water-quality instrumentation — the TROLL family. Level TROLL and Rugged TROLL pressure/level loggers, BaroTROLL barometric loggers, Aqua TROLL multiparameter sondes (600 and 700 series), smarTROLL handhelds, and the VuLink telemetry gateway. Software: VuSitu (mobile) and HydroVu (cloud).
**Documentation:** <https://in-situ.com/> — *note: the site returned HTTP 403 to automated requests on 2026-08-25; browse manually.*
**Band:** $–$$$.
**Southern Africa:** distributed through instrumentation dealers in South Africa; no dealer verified in this pass.
**Notes:** the Aqua TROLL 600 is a direct competitor to the YSI EXO2 with a smaller form factor; the vented Level TROLL 700 removes the barometric-correction problem entirely.

### OTT HydroMet (Germany/USA — includes Hydromet, Sutron, Lufft and Kipp & Zonen)
**Makes:** the widest hydrometeorological range of any single vendor. Precipitation: **OTT Pluvio²** weighing gauge and Parsivel² optical disdrometer. Water level: **OTT CBS bubbler**, RLS radar level sensor, PLS/PLS-C pressure probes, **OTT ecoLog 1000/1000 Ci** groundwater loggers, HydroSight camera-based systems. Discharge: **OTT MF pro** electromagnetic flow meter, **OTT ADC** acoustic Doppler current meter, SLD side-looking Doppler. Water quality: ecoN nitrate sensor. Loggers/telemetry: **netDL**, Sutron **XLink** and SatLink. Meteorology: Lufft **WS-series** compact all-in-one weather sensors; Kipp & Zonen **CMP/SMP pyranometers** and CHP1 pyrheliometer.
**Documentation:** <https://www.otthydromet.com/> (HTTP 200).
**Band:** $$–$$$$.
**Southern Africa:** OTT products appear across SADC national hydrological services; the Kipp & Zonen radiation line is widely distributed in South Africa through solar-industry instrumentation dealers.
**Notes:** the natural first-call vendor if you want a complete hydrometric station from one supplier with a single support relationship.

### Campbell Scientific (USA/UK — with an African subsidiary)
**Makes:** the reference research-grade data loggers and measurement/control systems, plus a large sensor catalogue. Verified current logger families on the product listing: **CR310**, **CR350**, **CR1000Xe**, plus the GRANITE modular measurement system and CRVW vibrating-wire loggers. Soil moisture (verified on the current listing): **CS616** water-content reflectometer, **CS650** and **CS655** water-content reflectometers, **TDR200** TDR system with TDR probes, and the SoilVUE profile probe. Also: EC-series eddy-covariance systems, weather stations, telemetry (cellular, satellite, radio), and LoggerNet software.
**Documentation:** <https://www.campbellsci.com/data-loggers> · <https://www.campbellsci.com/soil-moisture-sensors> (both HTTP 200).
**Band:** $$–$$$$.
**Southern Africa:** **Campbell Scientific Africa (Pty) Ltd — <https://www.campbellsci.co.za/> (HTTP 200, verified).** This is the single most useful regional presence in this entire register: local sales, support, calibration and training, in the same time zone and currency region.
**Notes:** if you are building a serious monitoring station in Namibia, start here. The CR310/CR350 with SDI-12 sensors is the standard modern configuration.

Also: **Model 401 Waterloo Multilevel System** and **Model 403 CMT Multilevel System** (both verified in the sitemap), water level meters (Model 101/102/107 TLC), interface meters (Model 122), discrete-interval and bladder samplers, peristaltic pumps, drive-point piezometers, and the FLUTe liner systems (**405** series).
**Documentation:** <https://www.solinst.com/> (HTTP 200).
**Band:** $–$$$.
**Southern Africa:** Solinst maintains a distributor network including South Africa; specific dealer not verified in this pass.
**Notes:** the SASSCAL Cuvelai groundwater programme used Solinst Leveloggers in its shallow Ohangwena monitoring boreholes — direct precedent for exactly this setting.

### Van Essen Instruments / Nova Metrix (Netherlands)
**Makes:** the **Diver** family of groundwater loggers — the other half of the borehole-logger duopoly with Solinst. Verified product pages: **TD-Diver** (temperature + level), **Baro-Diver** (barometric), **Cera-Diver** (ceramic, corrosion-resistant), **CTD-Diver** (conductivity, temperature, depth), **Micro-Diver** (16 mm diameter, for narrow standpipes). Telemetry and interfaces: **Diver-DXT**, **Diver-Gate M** and **Diver-Gate S**, **Diver-SDI**, **Diver-MOD**, **Diver-Link**. Software: **Diver-Field** and Diver-Office.
**Documentation:** <https://www.vanessen.com/> (HTTP 202) — product pages under `/products/data-loggers/…`.
**Band:** $–$$.

If you encounter "Schlumberger Diver" equipment in an existing Namibian monitoring network, Van Essen is the current support route.
**Notes:** the 16 mm Micro-Diver is the answer when the borehole has a narrow casing or a pump already installed.

## Drilling and pumping

### Grundfos (Denmark)
**Makes:** submersible borehole pumps — the **SP** series (the industry standard stainless multistage submersible, from small domestic to large irrigation), **SQ** and **SQE** (compact 3-inch domestic submersibles with integrated electronics and dry-run protection), and **SQFlex** — a renewable-energy submersible pump that runs directly from PV or wind over a wide DC/AC input range, in both centrifugal and helical-rotor variants. Also CR pressure pumps, IO101/IO50 controllers and the CU200 control unit.
**Documentation:** <https://www.grundfos.com/> — *the site did not complete an automated HTTPS request on 2026-08-25; browse manually.*
**Band:** $$–$$$.
**Southern Africa:** Grundfos has a South African subsidiary and a substantial Namibian dealer network — this is one of the easiest brands to buy, install and get serviced in Namibia.

### LORENTZ (Bernt Lorentz GmbH, Germany)
**Makes:** solar water pumping, and effectively the specialist in it. **PS2** series (PS2-100 to PS2-4000, with helical rotor and centrifugal pump ends), **PSk2** larger systems, surface pumps, **SmartSolution** pre-engineered kits, PumpScanner mobile app and **DataModule/CONNECTED** remote monitoring, plus the COMPASS sizing software.

**Southern Africa:** LORENTZ has a large and long-established dealer network across southern Africa including Namibia — solar pumping is a mature commercial market here.
**Notes:** use the free **COMPASS** sizing tool with your actual TDH and daily volume; it will produce a specific model and array recommendation and a monthly yield estimate, which is exactly the calculation in `05`.

## Geophysics

### Guideline Geo (Sweden — ABEM and MALÅ brands)
**Makes:** verified current product names from the company's own product listing: **ABEM Terrameter LS 2** (multi-channel resistivity and IP), **ABEM Terrameter VES**, **ABEM WalkTEM 2** (time-domain EM), **ABEM GroundTEM i / Trek / Ralli**, **ABEM Terraloc Pro 2** (seismograph), and the **MALÅ Ground Explorer** GPR family.
**Documentation:** <https://www.guidelinegeo.com/products/> (HTTP 200).
**Band:** $$$–$$$$.
**Southern Africa:** ABEM equipment is widely used by South African and Namibian groundwater consultants; several regional geophysical survey contractors own Terrameter systems, so **hiring a survey is usually far more sensible than buying a system**.
**Notes:** for the Cuvelai specifically, **WalkTEM 2 is arguably the more informative instrument than ERT**, because TEM resolves conductive (brackish) layers at depth exceptionally well and the fresh/brackish structure is the thing you need to map.

### IRIS Instruments (France)
**Makes:** verified product names from the company's own site: the **Syscal** resistivity/IP family — **Syscal Kid**, **Syscal Junior** (and switch versions), **Syscal R1**, **Syscal Pro** (and Pro switch), **Syscal Terra switch**, **Syscal Marine**, **Syscal Monitoring Unit**; the **Elrec** IP receivers — **Elrec Lite**, **Elrec Pro**, **Elrec Terra** with TIP transmitters; the **VIP** transmitter series; **T-VLF**; and Prosys data-processing software. Also NUMIS magnetic resonance sounding (MRS) — a direct groundwater-detection method.
**Documentation:** <https://www.iris-instruments.com/> (HTTP 200).
**Band:** $$$–$$$$.
**Notes:** NUMIS MRS is the only surface geophysical method that detects water *directly* (via proton magnetic resonance) rather than inferring it from resistivity — expensive and slow, but uniquely unambiguous where the resistivity interpretation is contested.

### Geometrics (USA, part of OYO)
**Makes:** verified product names from the company's own site: **Geode** exploration seismographs and **GeodeFlex**; **G-859** portable caesium magnetometer/gradiometer; **G-882** marine magnetometer; **MagArrow** (drone-mounted magnetometer); **Atom** distributed seismic nodes; and the **OhmMapper** capacitively-coupled resistivity system.
**Documentation:** <https://www.geometrics.com/> (HTTP 200).
**Band:** $$$–$$$$.
**Notes:** OhmMapper tows a capacitively-coupled array behind a vehicle or on foot with no electrodes to plant — very fast shallow resistivity profiling, and it works on dry sand and hardstanding where galvanic electrodes fail.

### GF Instruments (Czech Republic)
**Makes:** affordable field geophysics — **ARES II** resistivity/IP imaging system, **CMD** electromagnetic conductivity meters (CMD-Explorer, CMD-Mini), **PM-2** proton magnetometer, and MRS and seismic equipment.
**Documentation:** <https://www.gfinstruments.cz/> — *the site did not complete an automated HTTPS request on 2026-08-25; browse manually.*
**Band:** $$–$$$.
**Notes:** the price-conscious alternative to ABEM and IRIS; the CMD conductivity meters are excellent, quick reconnaissance tools for mapping salinity variation across a plot before committing to a resistivity line.

## How to actually procure in Namibia

1. **Start with Campbell Scientific Africa** (<https://www.campbellsci.co.za/>) for anything logger- or station-based. Same region, local support, and they carry or can source most third-party sensors.
2. **Hire geophysics, do not buy it.** A day of ERT or TEM survey from a Windhoek- or South Africa-based consultant costs a fraction of a system and comes with an interpreter who knows the local stratigraphy.
3. **Buy pumps locally.** Grundfos and LORENTZ both have real Namibian dealer networks. A pump you can get serviced beats a marginally better pump you cannot.
4. **Buy consumables in bulk and early.** Reagents, desiccant, batteries, cable and spare tipping-bucket screens are what actually stop a monitoring programme, and they have long lead times to Okongo.
5. **Ask every supplier for the calibration certificate** and the recommended recalibration interval, and budget for it.
6. **Specify the datum, the units and the file format** in the purchase order. A logger that exports a proprietary binary you cannot read in ten years' time is a liability.

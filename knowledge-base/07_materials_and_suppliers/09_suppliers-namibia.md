# Namibian building materials supply landscape

**Summary.** Namibian building materials retail is dominated by two groups — **Pupkewitz Megabuild** (which also operates Builders Warehouse Katutura, Megaboards and SpecTiles, and is the JV partner in Agrimark Namibia) and **Build it** (SPAR Group franchise, with roughly 23 Namibian stores) — plus **Cashbuild**, **BUCO**, **Agrimark** and a set of specialist trade suppliers for steel, aluminium, glass and cement. Northern Namibia is genuinely well served for standard materials: there are builders' merchants in Oshakati, Ongwediva, Ondangwa, Oshikango, Eenhana, Outapi, Okahao, Omuthiya and — significantly — **Okongo itself**. What the north does *not* have is specialist stock, structural fabrication or design services; those come from Windhoek or South Africa.

## Key facts

- **There is a Build it store in Okongo**: 462 Okongo Main Road, Okongo.
- **There is a Build it store in Eenhana**: Erf 1615, Industrial Area, Ohangwena Region.
- Pupkewitz Megabuild states **19 branches** nationally and **more than 20 000 line items**, "supplying from foundation to roof".
- SPAR Group's 2025 capital markets material records the Build it Western Region as **RSA 39 and Namibia 23** stores.
- **Ohorongo Cement** at Otavi and **Cheetah Cement** are the two Namibian cement producers.
- **Wispeco Namibia** (est. 1968, Frans Indongo Group) has warehouse and sales facilities in **Windhoek, Swakopmund and Ondangwa**.
- **Steel Force** has a branch in **Ondangwa** — the closest genuine steel merchant with cut-and-bend capability to Ohangwena Region.

## 5. Agrimark Namibia (Kaap Agri / KAL Group)

Agrimark Operations has served the Namibian market for more than a decade as a **joint venture with Pupkewitz Holdings**. Its stated footprint "stretches all the way from **Grünau to Ondangwa**".

Product offer: irrigation systems, gardening equipment, animal feeds, pet accessories, **quality building materials, hardware and fencing**, fuel and lubricants, outdoor and camping gear.

> ⚠️ The Agrimark Namibia website (agrimark.com.na) was **unreachable at 2026-08-25** — its TLS certificate expired that day. The individual branch list could not be verified. Ondangwa is confirmed as the northernmost point of the footprint by the KAL Group corporate page.

Agrimark's real value on a rural Namibian project is fencing, gates, water reticulation, tanks, pumps and irrigation — the "farm" side of a building project that a builders' merchant handles poorly.

## 6. Cymot

Cymot is an automotive, outdoor and industrial retailer rather than a builders' merchant, but it is the reliable source for tools, power tools, workshop equipment, PPE, workwear, safety footwear, abrasives and paints across the country.

Branch towns per cymot.com: **Windhoek** (Hilltop, City Centre, Northern Industrial, Katutura, Service Centre, 4x4 Fitment Centre, Telesales), **Gobabis**, **Keetmanshoop**, **Oshakati**, **Otjiwarongo**, **Rundu**, **Swakopmund**, **Tsumeb**, **Walvis Bay**.

Published Windhoek numbers include: City Centre 061 295 7000 / 083 295 7000; Northern Industrial 061 331 600 / 083 295 8550; Katutura 061 237 759 / 083 295 8450; Hilltop 083 295 8900; Service Centre 061 295 6705; Gobabis 083 295 8650. *Northern branch telephone numbers were not captured in this pass.*

**Cymot Oshakati is the practical answer to "where do I buy a replacement grinder / drill bits / safety boots" for a northern Namibian site.**

| **Ace Aluminium Namibia** | Windhoek; +264 61 255 332; info@acealuminiumnam.com | 25+ years. Design, manufacture and installation of aluminium windows and doors; repairs and maintenance; special orders including aluminium roller shutters, bullet-resistant glass and clear burglar bars. | **Glasfit Namibia (Pty) Ltd** | 7 fitment centres | ⚠️ **Automotive glass only** — windscreen replacement, chip repairs, mobile units. **Not** an architectural glass supplier. Do not specify them for building glazing |

**Wispeco Ondangwa is the key finding here** for a northern Namibian project: steel window and door frames — which are the standard, robust, low-maintenance choice for a remote building — can be sourced within the region rather than from Windhoek.

Architectural glass (float, laminated, toughened, IGUs) has **no verified Namibian supplier** in this research. In practice, glazing is either processed in Windhoek by aluminium fabricators or imported from South Africa. Treat glass as a long-lead item and design to standard sizes.

### Which suppliers actually serve Ohangwena / Okongo

**Serve Okongo directly:** Build it Okongo (the only merchant physically in the town verified here).

**Serve Ohangwena Region without freight from further afield:** Build it Eenhana, Build it Oshikango.

**Serve the region from the Oshana/Oshikoto cluster (Ondangwa–Ongwediva–Oshakati), a routine same-day or next-day delivery for most merchants:** Pupkewitz Megabuild (Ondangwa, Ongwediva, Oshakati, Outapi, Okahao), Build it (Ondangwa, Oshakati, Oshana), Cashbuild (Ondangwa, Oshakati, Oshikango), Steel Force (Ondangwa), Wispeco (Ondangwa), Cymot (Oshakati), Agrimark (Ondangwa).

**Require freight from Windhoek (or Tsumeb/Otjiwarongo):** Megaboards and SpecTiles ranges, BUCO, most specialist chemicals, architectural glass, structural steel fabrication, roof trusses (unless a northern fabricator is found), and anything imported.

> ⚠️ Every merchant listed above states or implies a delivery service, but **none of the northern branches published a delivery radius, a delivery charge, or a lead time**. These must be established by direct enquiry per branch. A "we deliver" claim on a store page is not a commitment to deliver to Okongo.

## Open questions

- **All Build it Namibian telephone numbers on buildit.co.za are corrupted** by a formatting error. Real numbers must be obtained per store.
- **The complete Build it Namibia store list (23 stores) has not been enumerated.** Twelve are identified here; the balance are unknown.
- **Agrimark Namibia's branch list is unverified** (site down on the access date).
- **BUCO's Namibian branch network beyond Windhoek is unverified**, as is whether BUCO's roof truss manufacturing operates in Namibia or only in South Africa.
- **Jumbo Charhan could not be verified at all.**
- **No delivery radius, delivery charge, minimum order or lead time was published by any Namibian merchant.** These are the numbers a project actually needs and they must be gathered by telephone.
- No Namibian architectural glass processor, block/brick yard, ready-mix plant, roofing roll-former or timber treatment plant was identified.
- Whether Pupkewitz Megabuild's "19 branches" figure predates recent openings (a 2026 news item refers to Megabuild coming to Gobabis) is unclear — the branch locator lists more than 19 outlets including the specialist stores.

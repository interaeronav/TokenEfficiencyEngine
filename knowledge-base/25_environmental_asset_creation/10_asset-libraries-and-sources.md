# Asset libraries and sources — a licence-stated register

**Summary.** Buying or downloading an asset is a licensing decision as much as a quality decision, and the licences differ far more than the marketing suggests. The distinction that matters most for a residential visualisation practice is **can I hand this file to my client?** — because almost every licence permits you to render the asset and sell the render, and almost none permits you to redistribute the asset file itself. This file is a register of where environmental assets actually come from, with licence, cost, format and URL stated, and with the Quixel Megascans/Fab transition dated as carefully as the available sources allow.

> ⚠️ **The client-handover test.** Before using any asset, ask: *if the client asks for the Unreal project file, does this licence permit it?* For CC0 sources the answer is always yes. For TurboSquid the answer is explicitly no without buying a licence per client. For Fab, Sketchfab and most marketplaces the answer is normally no. Decide this at the start of the project, not at handover.

## 1. The Quixel Megascans → Fab transition

This is the single most consequential licensing change in this field in recent years, and it is widely misreported. Here is what is verifiable as of 2026-08-25:

**What quixel.com states now:**
- The Megascans and Megaplants library is accessed **through Fab.com or Fab in the Epic Games Launcher**, with **"over 18,000 assets and counting"**.
- **"Assets bought on Fab are available under the Fab Standard License."** — i.e. Megascans are now *purchased*, not free by default.
- The **legacy Megascans library, Bridge and Mixer** remain accessible for users who cannot use Fab, but **"you can only download your previously acquired Megascans through the legacy library — you can't buy new assets."**
- Studio and project-based custom licences are available by contacting Quixel.

**What fab.com shows:** a Free Content section that includes Quixel Megascans items — so a rotating or partial free selection exists alongside the paid catalogue.

**What is not verifiable from the pages fetched:** the exact dates and terms of the transition. Epic's Fab launch announcement and the Fab legal pages are behind a JavaScript/bot challenge and could not be retrieved. The widely-reported sequence — Fab launching in late 2024, the entire Megascans library being made free to claim for a limited window, and the paid Fab Standard License model taking over afterwards — is consistent with what quixel.com says today but **the dates are `needs-verification`**.

**Practical guidance:**
1. If you had a Quixel account before the transition, **check the legacy library** — assets you previously acquired are still downloadable there and under their original terms.
2. Claim anything currently listed as free on Fab. Free-to-claim windows do not reopen.
3. For new work, budget for Megascans as a **paid per-asset purchase** under the Fab Standard License.
4. **Read the Fab Standard License before including a Megascans asset in anything you will hand to a client.** `needs-verification` on its redistribution terms.
5. For anything that must ship in a client deliverable, prefer **CC0 sources** (Poly Haven, ambientCG) or assets you made yourself.

## 3. Choosing a source — a decision procedure

1. **Will the asset file be handed to the client, or only rendered?**
   - Handed over → CC0 only (Poly Haven, ambientCG), or assets you made, or a licence explicitly permitting it (KitBash3D's full commercial licence; a per-client TurboSquid purchase).
   - Rendered only → most marketplaces are fine.
2. **Is it site-specific?** The Ohangwena tree species, the local sand, the local block wall — build these. No library has them.
3. **Is it generic and non-hero?** A wheelbarrow, a plastic chair, a gutter bracket — buy it, or model it in an hour.
4. **Is it a surface?** Try Poly Haven and ambientCG first (CC0, 8K, free). Megascans only if their scan is materially better and you can pay.
5. **Is it an HDRI?** Poly Haven, or capture your own (`02 §4`). Your own is always better for reference matching.
6. **Is it elevation or imagery?** Copernicus and SRTM first (free and safe); SkyFi when you need current, high-resolution or tasked imagery.
7. **Record it.** Every third-party asset goes into `ASSET_REGISTER.csv` (`08 §1`) with its source, URL, licence, cost, date and a redistributable yes/no flag. This is the document that answers a licence question two years later.

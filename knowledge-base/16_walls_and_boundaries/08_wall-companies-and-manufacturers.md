# Wall companies, manufacturers and suppliers

**Summary.** The southern African walling market divides into five supply chains: precast concrete panel-and-post walling (a fragmented industry of regional manufacturers), concrete masonry units and retaining blocks (Corobrik, Bosun, Technicrete/ISG, Infraset), palisade and mesh fencing (fabricators, mostly regional), gates and automation (dominated by Centurion Systems, with ET/Nice and DACE), and cement (Ohorongo in Namibia). This file records what was verifiable about each, and then gives the checklist of what to ask for when quoting — which matters more than the brand.

> ⚠️ **Status: draft.** Several southern African walling manufacturers' websites were unreachable in this research pass, and no price data was obtained for any product. Company details below are recorded only where a source was actually fetched. Everything unverified is marked `needs-verification`.

## 1. Precast concrete walling manufacturers

The panel-and-post boundary wall is a **regional, low-transport-radius product** — panels are heavy, low-value-per-tonne, and fragile, so every metropolitan area has its own manufacturers and the industry is fragmented. There is no national brand.

### Country Wide Walling
**Verified:** manufactures a full range of industrial and domestic precast walling and concrete palisade fencing, in **plain and decorative patterns**, with **single- and double-sided** units. Located at Plot 222, 7th Street, Witpoort, Brakpan, Gauteng 1540. Tel 081 740 5439, email cwwreception@yahoo.com.
**Not verified:** panel and post dimensions, available heights, standard post centres, delivery radius, whether they deliver into Namibia. `needs-verification`

### Cape Concrete
Cape Town precast manufacturer, generally associated with lintels, blocks and walling. **Website unreachable in this pass** — no product data verified. `needs-verification`

### Concrete Units
**Verified:** a Cape Town heavy-precast specialist, not a boundary-walling manufacturer. Their range is infrastructure-scale — stormwater pipes, culverts, road barriers, jacking pipes, 60-tonne wind-turbine tower segments, 40-tonne bridge beams, 25-tonne breakwater armour units, precast jetty components, concrete sea walls, stadium seats, river weir units, ship dry-docking support blocks, and **exposed aggregate façade units and panels**. Tel +27 21 386 1923.
**Relevance to a boundary wall:** none directly, but they are the reference point for architectural exposed-aggregate precast panels if a high-end feature wall is wanted.

### Coastal Walling

### Bosun
**Verified:** established **1993**, privately owned, with advanced factories in **Gauteng, North West and the Eastern Cape**. Named products: **Bosun Versoblock**, **Bosun Robust Retaining Wall Block**, and a 90° segmental concrete block retaining wall solution. **Bosun Colourfast®** is their pigment technology for permanent paving colour.

## 2. Palisade, mesh and fencing manufacturers

No manufacturer's site in this category was successfully fetched in this pass, so no product data is recorded. The categories to source are:

- **Steel palisade** — rolled D-section or W-section pales, or square tube, on I-section or SHS posts. Ask for **hot-dip galvanizing to SANS 121 (ISO 1461)**.
- **Welded mesh security fencing** — including anti-climb ("358") mesh panels.
- **Diamond mesh / chain link** and razor and barbed wire.
- **Concrete palisade** — often made by the same firms as precast walling.
- **Gabions and Reno mattresses** — **Maccaferri** is the originating and dominant international manufacturer, with a South African operation; the family produced sack gabions from 1893 and patented the box gabion in the early 20th century. Specify **PVC-coated galvanized** wire (estimated 60-year life) for permanent work.

### What else the gate needs
- **Remotes and receivers** — code-hopping only; fixed-code remotes are trivially cloned.
- **Safety infrared beams** across the opening — non-negotiable.
- **Intercom** — hard-wired or GSM. GSM intercoms are the practical choice where there is no structured cabling.
- **Keypads** and, increasingly, app-based control.
- **Battery and solar** — specify the battery capacity and, in Okongo, a small PV panel to maintain it.

## 6. What to ask for when quoting

This is the part of the file that transfers regardless of which company you use.

### For precast panel-and-post walling
1. **Exact panel dimensions** (length × height × thickness) and **exact post centres**. The post spacing is not adjustable.
2. **Post length, section, and required embedment depth**, and the manufacturer's recommended **hole size and concrete grade**.
3. **Panel reinforcement** — is the panel reinforced, and with what?
4. **Maximum wall height** the system is rated for, and on what basis.
5. **Wind loading** — has the system been designed for a stated wind pressure? Ask for the number.
6. **Pattern options**, single or double sided, and whether the pattern is on the visible side of *your* boundary.
7. **Capping** — included or extra?
8. **Delivery** — to site, off-loaded, or to the nearest depot? Panel breakage allowance? Who bears breakage in transit?
9. **Lead time**, and minimum order quantity.
10. **Installation** — by the manufacturer, by an approved installer, or by others? Warranty implications.
11. **Written warranty**, its duration, and what it excludes.
12. **Sample** — one panel and one post delivered before ordering.

### For masonry units
1. **SANS compliance** — SANS 1215 for concrete units, SANS 227 for clay. Ask for the certificate and the LOA/mark.
2. **Compressive strength** — the actual class, not "strong". Freestanding walls need ≥ 5,0 MPa solid / 3,0 MPa hollow (SANS 10400-K).
3. **Actual work-size dimensions and tolerance**, measured on 10 units, not the catalogue size.
4. **Units per m²** and **mortar per 1 000 units** for the actual size.
5. **Moisture expansion class** for clay units — it determines control-joint spacing (SANS 10400-K Table 19).
6. **Efflorescence grade** for clay units (SANS 227 Table 4).
7. **Delivery** — palletised or loose, and off-loading arrangements.
8. **Age** — concrete units should be at least 28 days old before being built in, to reduce shrinkage in the wall.

### For retaining wall blocks
1. **Block-on-block friction and nib shear strength** test results. Bosun publishes that they test for these; ask everyone.
2. **Design software or design tables**, and the design assumptions (soil φ', γ, surcharge).
3. **Maximum unreinforced height** and the geogrid schedule above it.
4. **Face angle options** and the effective vertical height per course at each angle.
5. **Base course and coping unit** availability.
6. Whether the supplier will **provide an engineer's design** for your wall or expects you to.

### For cement
1. **Strength class and type** — match the mix tables you are using (32,5 or 42,5).
2. **NSI / SABS certification** and, in South Africa, the **NRCS Letter of Authority number** (verifiable on 012 428 5199 or nrcs.org.za).
3. **Bag marking** — strength grade, composition notation and LOA number must be on the bag.
4. **Project pricing** for volume, with the total volume, type and duration.
5. **Delivery point** — for Okongo, the Ondangwa depot.
6. **Storage** — cement must be kept dry and off the ground; ask about pallet return.

### For gates and automation
1. **Actual gate mass**, weighed or calculated, not estimated from width.
2. **Motor model, duty cycle, and battery capacity**; confirm battery backup and expected cycles per charge.
3. **Safety devices included** — infrared beams as standard, not as an extra.
4. **Warranty and local service**. Who fixes it, from where, and in how long? For Okongo, this is the decisive question — a motor with no service presence north of Windhoek is the wrong motor.
5. **Spares availability** for rack, rollers, controller board and battery.
6. **Commissioning record** — force settings, limit positions, and a handover demonstration.

### General commercial questions
- Is the price **inclusive of VAT**, delivery, and off-loading?
- **Price validity period** — cement and steel prices move.
- **Payment terms** and whether a deposit is required.
- **What is excluded** — this is where the money is. Excavation? Spoil removal? Curing water? Making good?
- **Cross-border**: for South African supply into Namibia, who handles export documentation, duties, and transport, and what is the delivered price at site?

# European concealed-hinge standards - EN 15570 and EN 15828

European (cup) concealed cabinet hinges are covered by the EN 15570 and EN 15828 standard family, which governs hinge strength and durability testing for furniture hardware. The KB's hardware file cites manufacturer figures (Ø35 mm cup, 12.8 mm typical depth) without naming the governing standards; this entry adds the normative reference layer.

## Key facts

- Standard family: EN 15570 / EN 15828 (furniture hinge strength and durability)
- Standard cup boring: Ø35 mm; typical cup depth 12.8 mm (thin-door ~7.8 mm)
- Full in-situ 3-screw adjustability (side/height/depth) characterises the type

# Hardware systems — hinges, runners, box systems, lift systems and connectors

**Summary.** Hardware determines carcass dimensions, not the other way round. Choose the drawer system before fixing internal cabinet widths; choose the hinge before fixing door overlays and reveals; choose the lift system before fixing wall-cabinet heights. All the major systems — Blum, Hettich, Grass, Salice, Häfele — share the **Ø35 mm hinge cup** and the **32 mm system grid**, but their drawer box dimensions, drilling positions and panel-size formulas are proprietary and not interchangeable.

## Key facts

- Concealed hinge cup: **Ø35 mm**, typical cup depth **12.8 mm** (Hettich Sensys 8645i); thin-door variant 7.8 mm; zero-protrusion variant 11.6 mm.
- Hinge cup screw pattern: **45 mm** (TB) or **52 mm** (TH) spacing; Hettich TH 52 uses C 5.5 mm, TB 52 uses C 9.5 mm.
- LEGRABOX drawer sides: **N 66.5 / M 90.5 / K 128.5 / C 177 / F 241 mm**; 40 kg class NL 270–600 mm, 70 kg class NL 450–650 mm.
- TANDEMBOX antaro sides: **N 69 / M 84 / K 116 / C 171 / D 203 mm**; 30 kg class NL 270–600 mm, 65 kg class NL 450–650 mm.
- InnoTech Atira sides: **54 / 70 / 144 / 176 mm**; NL 260, 300, 350, 420, 470, 520, 620 mm; Quadro 25 = 25 kg partial extension, Quadro V6 = 30 kg full, Quadro V6+ = 50 kg.
- Häfele Slido D-Line11 sliding gear: **50, 80 or 120 kg** door weight classes.

## 1. Concealed cup hinges

**Geometry.** A concealed hinge is defined by four numbers: the **cup diameter** (Ø35 mm, universal), the **boring distance K** from the door edge to the near side of the cup (typically 3–8 mm; 5 mm is the common default, giving 22.5 mm from the edge to the cup centre), the **cranking** of the hinge arm, and the **mounting plate height D** (0, 1.5, 3, 5, 6, 9 mm). Together these set the overlay.

**Overlay types:**
- **Full overlay** — the door covers the whole carcass side. Straight (0-crank) arm.
- **Half overlay** — two doors share one carcass side. ~9.5 mm crank.
- **Inset** — the door sits inside the carcass. ~16 mm crank.

Increasing the mounting plate distance D *reduces* overlay; increasing the boring distance K *increases* overlay. Both interact, which is why every manufacturer publishes an overlay chart — use it rather than calculating.

**Opening angles.** 95° (thick doors, up to ~32 mm), **110° (the standard)**, 125°, 155°, 165° (zero-protrusion, needed where an internal drawer must clear the door), 170°, and blind-corner/bi-fold hinges at 50–65°. A 110° hinge on a full-overlay door does **not** clear an internal drawer box — that is what the 155°/165° hinges are for.

**Named systems:**
- **Blum CLIP top BLUMOTION** — soft close integrated in the hinge cup; CLIP-on arm for tool-free removal; available screw-on, INSERTA (press-in) and EXPANDO (expanding dowel, no screws). 110° is the workhorse; 155° for internal-drawer cabinets; 170° for full access.
- **Hettich Sensys 8645i** — 110°, integrated Silent System, cup depth 12.8 mm, door thickness 15–24 mm, overlay adjustment **+2 / −2 mm**, drilling patterns TH 52 × 5.5 mm and TB (45 mm). Companions: **8631i** at 95° for doors 15–32 mm, **8646i** for thin doors 10–19 mm (cup depth 7.8 mm), **8657i** at 165° zero-protrusion (cup depth 11.6 mm). Mounting plate distances 0.0 / 1.5 / 3.0 / 5.0 mm (cross plates), 0.5 / 3.0 mm (linear).
- **Grass Tiomos** and **Salice Series 200 / Silentia+** are the other two mainstream families; both use the Ø35 mm cup and are dimensionally similar but have their own plate heights and overlay charts.

**Hinges per door (practical rule):** 2 up to 900 mm door height / 9 kg; 3 up to 1600 mm; 4 up to 2000 mm; 5 above that. Add one hinge for doors over 600 mm wide, for heavy glass or solid-timber doors, and for anything in a hot, dry climate where a solid door may cup slightly.

**Adjustment.** Three axes: depth (in/out, cam on the arm), height (mounting plate slot), and lateral/overlay (cam screw). Adjust in that order — depth, height, then overlay — and set all doors of a run to the same reveal (3 mm typical) before touching the drawers.

## 2. Drawer runners and box systems

### Blum

**LEGRABOX** — thin-walled (12.8 mm) steel sides, the premium system.

| Height code | Side height (mm) |
|---|---|
| N | 66.5 |
| M | 90.5 |
| K | 128.5 |
| C | 177 |
| F | 241 |

Load classes: **40 kg** (nominal lengths 270–600 mm) and **70 kg** (450–650 mm). Nominal lengths: 270, 350, 400, 450, 500, 550, 600 mm (plus 650 in the 70 kg class). Panel formulas: **drawer bottom width = internal cabinet width − 35 mm**; **drawer back width = internal cabinet width − 38 mm**. Variants: LEGRABOX *pure* (plain side) and *free* (glass/panel inserts). Motion options: BLUMOTION soft close, TIP-ON for handleless, TIP-ON BLUMOTION (mechanical push-to-open plus soft close), SERVO-DRIVE (electric).

**TANDEMBOX antaro** — the volume-market box system.

| Height code | Side height (mm) |
|---|---|
| N | 69 |
| M | 84 |
| K | 116 |
| C | 171 |
| D | 203 |

Load classes **30 kg** (NL 270–600 mm) and **65 kg** (NL 450–650 mm). Heights above M are made up with gallery rails or a design side rather than a taller steel side.

**MOVENTO** — concealed undermount runner for wooden or custom drawer boxes.

| Model | Load | Nominal lengths (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| 760H | 40 kg | 270, 300, 320, 350, 380, 420, 450, 480, 500, 520, 550, 600 |
| 766H | 60–70 kg | 450, 500, 520, 550, 580, 600, 650, 700, 750 |

Box formulas: **SKW (internal drawer width) = LW (internal cabinet width) − 42 mm** (+0 / −1.5 mm); **SKL (drawer length) = NL − 10 mm**. Drilling template T65.1000.02. Use MOVENTO where a timber or ply drawer box is wanted with metal-system motion.

### Hettich

**InnoTech Atira** — side heights **54, 70, 144, 176 mm**; nominal lengths 260, 300, 350, 420, 470, 520, 620 mm; carcase widths 275–1200 mm. Runners: **Quadro 25** (25 kg, partial extension), **Quadro V6** (30 kg, full extension), **Quadro V6+** (50 kg, full extension), each with Silent System or Push to open Silent.

Panel formulas, where `LB` = carcase internal width and `EB` = the installation width deduction that depends on carcase panel thickness `KD`:

| KD (panel thickness) | EB |
|---|---|
| 16 mm | 12.5 mm |
| 18 mm | 10.5 mm |
| 19 mm | 9.5 mm |

- **Bottom panel width = LB − 2 × EB − 51.5 mm**
- **Rear panel width = LB − 2 × EB − 63 mm**

**AvanTech YOU** is Hettich's premium thin-side system (the LEGRABOX competitor) and **ArciTech** the modular platform; both use the same Quadro-family runner logic.

### Grass

**Nova Pro Scala** — stepped-side drawer system with drawer side heights including **63 mm and 90 mm**, in **40 kg and 70 kg** load classes, with soft-close and push-to-open options. **Dynapro** is Grass's synchronised full-extension concealed undermount runner for custom boxes.

### Choosing between them

- Kitchen base units with pots and pans: 70 kg class, C or F height (LEGRABOX) or 176 mm (Atira) with gallery.
- Cutlery and utensil drawers: N or M height, 40 kg, with an internal divider system.
- Wardrobe internal drawers: 40 kg, M or K height, 450–500 mm NL.
- Timber or ply drawer boxes: MOVENTO or Dynapro undermount.
- Budget or garage-store work: side-mounted ball-bearing runners (35–45 kg, 250–600 mm) — cheap, tolerant of dust and misalignment, visible.

> ⚠️ Load ratings are **dynamic** ratings for the runner pair under test conditions. Derate for long unsupported fronts, for stone or solid-timber fronts, and for a shop that is not achieving 0.5 mm assembly accuracy.

## 3. Lift systems

Blum **AVENTOS** is the reference family. Selection is by *power factor* = cabinet height × door weight (including twice the handle weight for stay-lift types); each mechanism covers a power-factor band, and the manufacturer's calculator or Excel planning tool should be used.

| System | Motion | Cabinet height range | Front width range |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HF** | bi-fold (two-part front folds up) | 479–1067 mm | 381–1828 mm |
| **HS** | up-and-over (single front lifts over the top) | 350–800 mm | 381–1828 mm |
| **HL** | lift-up parallel to the cabinet | 300–580 mm (appliance garage 450–580 mm) | 381–1828 mm |
| **HK top** | stay lift, single front | 300–610 mm | 381–1828 mm |
| **HK-S** | compact stay lift | 186–400 mm | 381–991 mm |

Mounting uses Ø5 mm locating pin holes at 5 mm depth on the 32 mm grid. Hettich's equivalents are the **Free flap / Free fold / Free up** family; Häfele markets **Free flap** under licence and its own **Duo/Senso** lift fittings.

**Design consequence:** a bi-fold or up-and-over front cannot be scribed to a ceiling and needs clear swing space; check the arc against a rangehood, a window reveal and the user's head height before committing.

## 4. Sliding and folding door gear

- **Häfele Slido D-Line11** — furniture and room-door sliding gear in **50 / 80 / 120 kg** classes, wall, ceiling or floor mounted, 1–2 doors per system, with soft-close options; minimum door widths 500 mm (basic), 517 mm (single soft close), 556 mm (ball-bearing), 780 mm (dual soft close); height adjustment ±4 mm. Glass door options for 8, 10, 12, 12.7 mm toughened and 8–8.7, 10–10.7, 13 mm laminated.
- **Blum SPACE STEP / MERIVOBOX / REVEGO** — REVEGO is Blum's pocket-door system for concealing a whole run (relevant to sculleries and home offices).
- **Folding gear** — bi-fold and multi-fold door fittings for wardrobes where sliding gear loses too much opening width.
- **Wardrobe sliding rule:** a two-door slider gives access to only half the opening at a time and loses ~50 mm of internal depth to the track. Hinged doors need 900 mm of swing space but give full access. In a dry, dusty region, specify bottom-rolling gear with a brush-sealed top guide, or top-hung gear with a floor guide that cannot fill with grit.

## 5. Handles, locks, feet and connectors

- **Handles:** bar handles are specified by **centre-to-centre** dimension (96, 128, 160, 192, 224, 320, 480 mm are the common ones — all multiples that suit the 32 mm grid). Use a drilling jig. Handleless options: J-pull/finger-pull profiles, integrated rail profiles, TIP-ON and TIP-ON BLUMOTION push-to-open, or Blum SERVO-DRIVE.
- **Locks:** cam locks (Ø19 or 22 mm hole), espagnolette for tall doors, and push locks for drawers. For a garage store, a hasp-and-staple over a proper carcass is more secure than a furniture lock.
- **Adjustable feet:** typically 100–150 mm with an M10 thread and a Ø25–30 mm mounting plate, plus clip-on plinth brackets. Always four per cabinet, five for widths over 900 mm.
- **Cabinet connectors:** cabinet-to-cabinet screws with a plastic sleeve (Ø5 mm, 30–40 mm), fitted through pre-drilled system holes; two per joint minimum, three for tall units.
- **Knock-down connectors:** Lamello **Clamex P-14** (up to 1210 N in MDF, 1070 N in particleboard, 1870 N in beech; minimum 18 mm butt / 15 mm mitre; needs a Ø6 mm access hole), **Tenso P-14** (self-clamping, ~15 kg clamping force, max 30-minute glue window), **Divario P-18** (invisible shelf connector, up to 600 N), **Bisco P** (alignment only). Cam-and-dowel (minifix/Rafix) remains the cheapest KD option.
- **Shelf supports:** Ø5 mm pins for light shelves; for spans over 800 mm in 18 mm board use a 25 mm+ front lipping or a mid support, or the shelf will sag visibly within a year.

## 6. Specifying hardware — a checklist

1. State manufacturer, product family, **model designation**, finish and quantity per item — "Blum LEGRABOX pure, height C (177 mm), NL 500 mm, 70 kg, BLUMOTION, orion grey" is a specification; "soft-close drawers" is not.
2. State the **carcass panel thickness** the hardware is calculated for (16, 18 or 19 mm). Changing panel thickness changes every drawer panel dimension.
3. State hinge type, opening angle, overlay and mounting-plate distance per door type.
4. State the drilling pattern (TH 52 / TB 45) if the shop is boring rather than using a CNC library.
5. Confirm **local availability and lead time** before specifying. **[NA]** Blum, Hettich and Häfele are all distributed into Namibia through South African agents; lead times on non-stock items (LEGRABOX in a non-standard finish, AVENTOS mechanisms) are the commonest cause of programme slip on a joinery package.
6. Allow a **5 % spares** allowance on hinges, plates and runner clips.

## Open questions

- **Salice** technical data (Series 200 cup depth, plate heights, overlay charts) could not be retrieved — the manufacturer's product pages returned 404. Verify from a current Salice catalogue before specifying.
- **Grass Nova Pro Scala** side heights: only 63 mm and 90 mm were confirmed from Häfele product listings; the taller variants (122 mm, 186 mm and railing systems) were not verified, nor were the panel-size formulas.
- **Blum CLIP top BLUMOTION** boring distances and mounting-plate distance tables were not obtained from Blum's own technical catalogue; the values here for boring distance (3–8 mm) come from a secondary source. Use the Blum overlay chart for any production setting-out.
- AVENTOS power-factor bands are published in imperial units in the source consulted; use Blum's own calculator for metric selection.
- Handle centre-to-centre ranges and hinges-per-door rules are trade practice, not manufacturer-published limits.

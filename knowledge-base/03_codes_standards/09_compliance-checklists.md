# Compliance checklists — residential plan submission, inspection stages and project document register

**Summary.** Four executable checklists: a residential plan-submission checklist for **[ZA]**, one for **[NA]**, an inspection-stage checklist usable in either country, and a document register listing everything a compliant project file should contain. Each item is written as a discrete, checkable action. Items marked *verify locally* vary by municipality and must be confirmed against that authority's own checklist before submission.

> ⚠️ These checklists are a working tool, not a legal opinion. Every local authority in both countries maintains its own submission checklist and its own drawing conventions; obtain it and reconcile before lodging.

## A. Residential plan-submission checklist **[ZA]** South Africa

### A.1 — Before you draw

- [ ] Confirm the **erf number, township and street address**, and obtain the **title deed** and the **SG diagram**.
- [ ] Obtain a **zoning certificate** from the municipality: zoning, permitted use, coverage, floor area ratio, height, building lines, parking requirement, and any title deed restrictive conditions or servitudes.
- [ ] Confirm whether a **land use application** (rezoning, consent use, departure, removal of restrictions) is required under SPLUMA and the municipal land use scheme, and lodge it **before** the building plan — building plans cannot be approved contrary to the scheme.
- [ ] Check for special overlays: heritage (a building over 60 years old triggers a SAHRA/PHRA permit for alteration or demolition), coastal management, environmental sensitivity, dolomite land, mining-affected land, urban edge.
- [ ] Determine the **occupancy classification** under Regulation A20 (H4 dwelling house; H3 domestic residence; H5 hospitality; etc.).
- [ ] Determine whether the building is a **category 1 building** (class A3, A4, F2, G1, H2, H3 or H4; no basement; ≤ 6,0 m between intersecting walls or lateral supports; ≤ 80 m² floor area).
- [ ] Determine whether the work is **minor building work** under the Regulation definition — if so, apply to the **building control officer** for authorisation instead of lodging full plans.
- [ ] Establish the **SANS 10400-XA energy zone** from the current edition's town table.
- [ ] Establish the **site class** and whether a geotechnical investigation is required (NBR Regulation F3 / NHBRC).

### A.2 — Appointments

- [ ] Appoint the designer. Confirm registration under the **Architectural Profession Act 44 of 2000** in a category permitting the scope of work.
- [ ] Identify every element requiring a **rational design or rational assessment** — foundations outside SANS 10400-H, wall panels outside SANS 10400-K, roof outside SANS 10400-L, retaining walls, pools, fire engineering under T1(2), stormwater under R3, drainage under P2(2), non-water-borne sanitation under Q3, artificial ventilation under O4, structural support from an existing building under A1(3), excavation stability under G1(3), fire installation under W4, energy by simulation under XA3.
- [ ] For each, **appoint an approved competent person under Regulation A19** — the appointment is made by the **owner**, in writing, stating that the person undertakes all duties required by the Regulations.
- [ ] Obtain a completed **Form 2** (application for acceptance as an approved competent person) from each.
- [ ] Obtain **Form 3** declarations from any competent person designing only a component or element of a system.
- [ ] Confirm each competent person's **professional indemnity insurance** is current and adequate.

### A.3 — Drawings and documents to lodge

- [ ] **Application form** on the local authority's own form, signed by the owner (or agent under power of attorney) — Act s 4(3).
- [ ] **Form 1** — declaration by the person responsible for preparing the application under s 4.
- [ ] **Site / block plan** showing the erf, all buildings existing and proposed, building lines, servitudes, boundary distances, levels, access, and north point. *Scale — verify locally; typically 1:200 or 1:500.*
- [ ] **Layout drawing indicating occupancy classification** (required by Regulation A2).
- [ ] **Floor plans** of every storey, dimensioned, with room names, areas and window/door schedules. *Typically 1:100.*
- [ ] **Sections** — at least one through the wettest and most complex part of the building, showing floor, wall and roof construction, damp-proofing, heights and levels.
- [ ] **Elevations** — all four, showing finished ground level, natural ground level, boundary walls and materials.
- [ ] **Drainage layout** — sewer connection point, inspection eyes, gullies, rodding eyes, manholes, invert levels, pipe sizes and falls; septic tank / conservancy tank / french drain layout with percolation test results where there is no sewer.
- [ ] **Stormwater layout** — roof drainage, downpipe discharge, surface falls, discharge point.
- [ ] **Structural drawings and calculations** where a rational design applies, signed by the competent person.
- [ ] **Roof truss layout and design certificate** from the truss manufacturer's engineer.
- [ ] **SANS 10400-XA energy schedule** — zone, orientation, envelope R-values or CR-values, fenestration percentage or U-value/SHGC calculation, hot water compliance statement.
- [ ] **Geotechnical report and site class certificate** where required.
- [ ] **Specification** of materials and workmanship.
- [ ] **Fire schedule** where the occupancy is other than a simple H4 — occupancy class, population, escape route travel distances, separating element ratings, hose reel and extinguisher schedule.
- [ ] **Consents**: neighbour consent where a building line relaxation or a boundary wall is proposed; body corporate consent in a sectional title scheme; homeowners' association approval where applicable.
- [ ] **Heritage permit** where applicable.
- [ ] **Environmental authorisation** where a NEMA listed activity is triggered.
- [ ] **Proof of payment** of the plan scrutiny fee. *Amount — verify locally.*
- [ ] Confirm the **number of copies** and whether electronic lodgement is required. *Verify locally.*

### A.4 — NHBRC (new homes built by a registered home builder for a housing consumer)

- [ ] Confirm the builder holds a current **NHBRC registration certificate**.
- [ ] Lodge **enrolment at least 15 days before commencement of construction**.
- [ ] Enrolment form **EF003** (or ST003 for sectional title), signed with barcode.
- [ ] **Appendix B1** competent person certification.
- [ ] **Appendix B3** site soil class designation.
- [ ] **Geotechnical report** — a comprehensive report for problem soil types H2, H3, C2, S2 and P.
- [ ] **SANAS-accredited compaction test results** where bulk earthworks are required.
- [ ] **Stormwater management report** for affected sites.
- [ ] **B4 certificate** and Council for Geoscience letter of comments for dolomitic sites (D1–D3); for D4, Phase 1 and 2 geotechnical reports, **two independent peer reviews** by Level 4 competent persons, a municipal risk management plan and professional indemnity insurance.
- [ ] Housing consumer index form and ID/passport copy; builder registration certificate and certified ID copy; cost breakdown and signed contract; title deed, deed of sale or Permission to Occupy; bank rating letter.
- [ ] Drawings signed by the registered professional.

### A.5 — After approval

- [ ] Confirm approval is in writing (Act s 4(1)) and note any conditions.
- [ ] Note the **decision period** applied: 30 days for buildings under 500 m²; 60 days for 500 m² and over (s 7(1)).
- [ ] If refused, check the reasons against s 7(1)(b) and consider **appeal under s 9** to the Review Board.
- [ ] Confirm any **time limit on erection** imposed under s 11.
- [ ] Notify the local authority of **commencement** as required by the municipal by-law. *Verify locally.*

## B. Residential plan-submission checklist **[NA]** Namibia

> ⚠️ **Step B.0 decides everything else.** Establish the tenure and jurisdiction before anything else, because in a communal area most of the rest of this checklist does not apply.

### B.0 — Establish jurisdiction and tenure first

- [ ] Determine whether the site is:
  - **(i) inside a proclaimed local authority area** (municipality, town council or village council) — full checklist below applies;
  - **(ii) inside a declared settlement area** under the Regional Councils Act 22 of 1992 — regional council administers; confirm what building approval, if any, it requires;
  - **(iii) on communal land** — Communal Land Reform Act 5 of 2002 applies; **there is no building plan approval regime**; or
  - **(iv) on commercial farmland** — Agricultural (Commercial) Land Reform Act 6 of 1995; no building control.
- [ ] For (i): obtain the **Government Notice proclaiming the local authority** and the surveyed boundary, and confirm the site falls inside it.
- [ ] For (iii): confirm the **customary land right or right of leasehold** is allocated by the Chief or Traditional Authority (s 22), **ratified by the regional Communal Land Board** (s 24) and **registered with a certificate issued** (s 25). For any institutional or commercial building, obtain a **right of leasehold** under ss 30–36, not a customary land right — a leasehold is registrable and supports finance.
- [ ] Confirm the **size limit** applicable to the customary land right (20 ha under the regulations, subject to ministerial adjustment).

### B.1 — Statutory and municipal enquiries (proclaimed areas)

- [ ] Ask the council for: the **Government Notice number and date of its current building regulations**; the list of **standard publications incorporated by reference under LAA s 94B**; and its **building plan submission checklist**.
- [ ] Exercise the **LAA s 94B(4)** right: inspect the incorporated standard publications **free of charge at the council's offices** to verify any clause you need.
- [ ] Obtain the **zoning scheme** position under the Urban and Regional Planning Act 5 of 2018: zoning, coverage, height, building lines, parking. Note that outside a local authority area the **Minister**, not a council, decides rezoning (URPA s 56) and most other applications.
- [ ] Confirm whether a **subdivision, consolidation or rezoning** application is needed and lodge it first.
- [ ] Obtain the **title deed** and general plan / SG diagram.
- [ ] Confirm the **current gazetted plan fee tariff** (LAA s 30(1)(u)) — do not rely on published historic figures.

### B.2 — Appointments

- [ ] If a professional is preparing the plans, confirm registration and obtain the **registration number**:
  - architect or quantity surveyor — **NCAQS**, Architects' and Quantity Surveyors' Act 13 of 1979;
  - engineer — **Engineering Council of Namibia**, Engineering Profession Act 18 of 1986 (Professional Engineer, Incorporated Engineer or Engineering Technician as appropriate to the reserved work).
- [ ] Note that Windhoek (and most councils) accept plans from **the owner**, or from a registered architect, surveyor or professional engineer quoting a registration number.
- [ ] Appoint a structural engineer where **structural computations** are required — Windhoek Reg 6 requires computations proving sufficient strength of structures and materials.

### B.3 — Drawings and documents to lodge (Windhoek pattern)

- [ ] **Application form** completed by the owner or an agent under **formal power of attorney**.
- [ ] **Building plan submission form** fully completed: erf number; township; sectional title addition yes/no; description of work; street from which entrance is obtained; construction materials (foundation, walls, damp course, roof cover); **ventilation percentage and window area per room**; swimming pool volume in m³; building value in N$; total built-up area, individual dwelling areas, outbuilding areas and total new additional area; owner's name, postal address, telephone, email, signature and date; designer's details, professional title, **registration number**, signature and date.
- [ ] **Plans in duplicate** (Windhoek Reg 6).
- [ ] **Block plan at not less than 1:1 000** showing the building's relation to other structures and to the streets.
- [ ] **Plans, sections and elevations at not less than 1:100** showing: height and thickness of foundations and walls; ground floor level; positions of the damp course; dimensions and positions of rooms and passages; position, form and dimensions of all windows, doors, chimneys and ventilating openings; sanitary conveniences and the intended mode of drainage.
- [ ] **Schedule of specifications** describing materials and methods.
- [ ] **Structural computations** proving sufficient strength of structures and materials.
- [ ] **Owner's (or builder's) signature on all plans.**
- [ ] **Proof of payment** of the gazetted fee — plans are not accepted until the form is complete and fees are paid.
- [ ] **Consent Form for Building Line Relaxation** where applicable.

### B.4 — Parallel approvals (apply everywhere in Namibia, including communal land)

- [ ] **Environmental Clearance Certificate** — screen the project against the Listed Activities (GN 29 of 2012). Triggers commonly missed on rural projects: opening a **borrow pit or sand-winning site** (activity 3.2), **groundwater abstraction for commercial purposes** (8.1), a **wastewater treatment plant** (8.6), **road construction** (10.1(b)), **rezoning** (5.1). Apply via https://eia.meft.gov.na/ (N$300). **An ECC is valid for three years.**
- [ ] **Water** — bulk from **NamWater** (Oshakati office for Ohangwena); reticulation from the local authority; rural schemes via the **Directorate of Rural Water Supply**; borehole drilling and abstraction permits under the Water Resources Management Act 11 of 2013.
- [ ] **Electricity** — apply to the regional distributor (**NORED** in northern Namibia including Ohangwena; **CENORED** in the central-north; **Erongo RED** in Erongo). Start the connection application early; wayleaves and line construction are usually the critical path. An electrical certificate to **SANS 10142-1** is required before energising.
- [ ] **Occupational health and safety** — Labour Act 11 of 2007 and the health and safety regulations (GN 156 of 1997). Prepare an HSE plan; on a contract-driven project expect the South African Construction Regulations or ISO 45001 to be imposed contractually.
- [ ] **Contractor** — confirm registration/grading where the client requires it (Construction Industries Federation of Namibia grading is common on tendered work).

### B.5 — Approval, commencement and completion

- [ ] Note the decision period — Windhoek Reg 8: **28 days** from receipt, extended to **35 days** where public holidays intervene.
- [ ] Note that approval **lapses if construction has not commenced within 12 months** (Windhoek Reg 9).
- [ ] Note that the building must be **completed within 12 months of commencement** (Windhoek Reg 10).
- [ ] Book **mandatory inspections** by a Council-authorised agent.
- [ ] Obtain the **Certificate of Occupation** before occupying.
- [ ] Obtain the **building compliance certificate** — required by **LAA s 78** before the Registrar of Deeds will register any transfer of the property.
- [ ] Remember **Windhoek Reg 3**: Council approval and inspection does **not** relieve the owner of full responsibility for the building.

## C. Inspection-stage checklist

Applies in both countries; **[ZA]** stages are those a BCO typically calls, **[NA]** stages are those a council-authorised agent typically calls. *The exact list and the notice period required vary by authority — verify locally.*

| # | Stage | What is checked | Hold point? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Setting out / site handover** | Building lines, boundary distances, levels against the approved site plan, site enclosure and public protection (NBR Part F) | Yes — before excavation |
| 2 | **Excavation / foundation trench, before concrete** | Trench depth, width and bottom; founding material against the geotechnical assumption; stability of the excavation (SANS 10400-G: permanent cuttings not steeper than 1:2, no ponding within 3 m of the toe); reinforcement where a rational design applies | **Yes — critical hold point** |
| 3 | **Foundation concrete** | Concrete grade, placing, cover to reinforcement | Sometimes |
| 4 | **Surface bed, before concrete** | Fill compaction and layer thickness; **damp-proof membrane** continuity and laps; termite treatment where specified; sleeved services penetrations; reinforcement | **Yes** |
| 5 | **Damp-proof course** | DPC continuity at wall base, at abutments, over openings, cavity trays and weep holes; DPC at least 150 mm above finished ground level *(verify against the current SANS 10400-K/J)* | Yes |
| 6 | **Drainage, before backfill** | Pipe size, gradient, bedding, joints, rodding access, inspection eyes and gullies; **hydraulic/air test** of the drainage installation (SANS 10400-P); septic tank / french drain construction and percolation test evidence | **Yes — before covering** |
| 7 | **Wall plate / roof structure before covering** | Wall panel dimensions against SANS 10400-K or the rational design; lintel bearings; control and articulation joints; **roof tie-down and anchorage**; truss layout against the certified truss design; bracing; timber grade marks and preservative treatment marks (SANS 1783, SANS 1288) | **Yes — critical hold point** |
| 8 | **Roof covering and waterproofing** | Underlay, fixings, flashings, falls to outlets on flat roofs, overflow provision | Yes |
| 9 | **Fire separations before closing up** | Party wall continuity to the underside of the roof covering or 500 mm above a combustible covering (SANS 10400-T 4.57.4); fire-stopping at service penetrations; protected shafts | **Yes — before ceilings** |
| 10 | **Electrical first fix and second fix** | Wiring to SANS 10142-1; earthing and bonding; earth leakage; final **Certificate of Compliance** from a registered electrician | Yes |
| 11 | **Plumbing pressure test** | Hot and cold water installation to SANS 10252-1; geyser installation to SANS 10254 (safety valve, vacuum breaker, drip tray and tray drain, pipe insulation) | Yes |
| 12 | **Glazing** | Safety glazing to SANS 1263-1 in every position listed in SANS 10400-N 4.4.2; **installer's permanent marking visible after installation** | Yes |
| 13 | **Energy compliance** | Insulation type, thickness and continuity against the declared R-value; fenestration areas as approved; hot water system as declared (XA2 50 % non-resistance) | Yes |
| 14 | **Final / occupancy inspection** | Whole building against the approved plans; all certificates lodged | **Yes — precondition of the certificate** |

**Certificates that must exist before the final inspection:**

- **[ZA]** **Form 4** certificates of completion of the **structural**, **fire protection** and **fire installation** systems from the appointed competent persons (Act s 14(2A)); the **electrical Certificate of Compliance**; a gas certificate of conformity where gas is installed; a plumbing certificate where the municipality requires one; an electric fence certificate where applicable.
- **[NA]** Equivalent professional certificates as required by the council, plus the electrical certificate required by the distributor.

## D. Document register — what a compliant project file contains

Organise the file in these ten sections. An agent auditing a project file should be able to tick every applicable line.

### D.1 Land and tenure

- Title deed / deed of transfer, or **[NA]** the registered customary land right certificate or right of leasehold
- SG diagram / general plan / cadastral survey
- Zoning certificate or zoning scheme extract
- Title deed restrictive conditions and servitude documents
- Land use application and approval (rezoning, consent use, departure, subdivision, township establishment)
- **[NA]** Communal Land Board ratification and registration documents; Traditional Authority allocation record

### D.2 Statutory approvals

- Building plan application form and receipted fee
- **Approved building plans** (stamped set)
- Written approval letter and any conditions
- **[ZA]** Form 1 declaration; minor building work authorisation where applicable
- Heritage permit (SAHRA / PHRA)
- Environmental authorisation **[ZA]** / **Environmental Clearance Certificate [NA]** and the EMP
- Water use authorisation / borehole permit
- Electricity connection agreement and wayleaves
- Neighbour consents, body corporate or HOA approvals
- Demolition permit where applicable

### D.3 Appointments and competence

- **[ZA]** Regulation A19 appointment letters from the **owner** for every competent person
- **[ZA]** Form 2 (acceptance as approved competent person) and Form 3 (component designer declarations)
- Professional registration certificates and current registration confirmations (SACAP, ECSA, SACNASP / **[NA]** NCAQS, ECN)
- Professional indemnity insurance certificates
- Contractor registration: **[ZA]** NHBRC registration certificate, CIDB grading where applicable; **[NA]** CIF grading where required
- Consultant appointment agreements and scopes of service

### D.4 Design record

- Design brief and design criteria statement (including the **assumed wind speed, seismic assumption and site class** — critical **[NA]** where no national map exists)
- Structural calculations and rational design reports, signed
- Geotechnical investigation report and site class certificate
- Roof truss design certificate and layout from the truss manufacturer's engineer
- Fire engineering rational design report where applicable (SANS 10400-T Annex B)
- Energy compliance report / XA schedule and any thermal simulation output with the software certification
- Drainage and stormwater design calculations
- Agrément South Africa certificates for any non-standard system
- Design change register and revised drawings with revision clouds

### D.5 Specifications and contract

- Full specification of materials and workmanship
- Bills of quantities
- Building contract (JBCC, NEC, FIDIC or equivalent) and all addenda
- Programme and any approved extensions of time

### D.6 Construction records

- Site instruction register
- Request for information (RFI) register
- Site meeting minutes
- Inspection records against the stage checklist in section C, signed
- Non-conformance register and close-outs
- Photographic record of covered work — **especially the DPM, drainage before backfill, roof tie-downs and fire-stopping**

### D.7 Materials and testing

- Delivery notes and batch certificates for cement (SANS 50197-1 / **[NA]** GN 259 compulsory specification), reinforcement, aggregate (SANS 1083)
- Concrete cube test results
- Compaction test results (SANAS-accredited where NHBRC requires)
- Timber grade stamps and preservative treatment certificates (SANS 1783, SANS 1288)
- Masonry unit certificates (SANS 227 / SANS 1215)
- Glazing certificates and evidence of SANS 1263-1 marking
- Waterproofing system data sheets, applicator certification and warranty
- Drainage pressure/air test records

### D.8 Statutory completion certificates

- **[ZA]** Form 4 — structural system
- **[ZA]** Form 4 — fire protection system
- **[ZA]** Form 4 — fire installation
- Electrical Certificate of Compliance (SANS 10142-1)
- Gas certificate of conformity
- Plumbing certificate (where required by the municipality)
- Electric fence system certificate
- Lift certificate where applicable
- **[ZA]** **Certificate of occupancy** (Act s 14)
- **[NA]** **Certificate of Occupation** and **building compliance certificate** (needed for LAA s 78 transfer)

### D.9 Warranties and insurance

- **[ZA]** NHBRC enrolment certificate and warranty record (3 months technical non-compliance; 1 year roof leaks; 5 years major structural defects)
- Manufacturer and installer warranties: roof covering, waterproofing, glazing, doors and ironmongery, geyser, solar system
- Contractor's all-risk insurance, public liability, works insurance
- Latent defects liability period record and defects list

### D.10 Handover

- As-built drawings
- Operation and maintenance manuals
- Health and safety file **[ZA]** (OHS Act Construction Regulations) / HSE close-out file **[NA]**
- Keys and access register
- Municipal account transfer / opening of services accounts
- Retention release record

## Open questions

- **The City of Windhoek Building Plan Submission Check List (.xls)** was identified but its contents were not extracted (spreadsheet format). Download and reconcile against section B.3.
- **[ZA]** The **statutory inspection stages** are not specified in the Act or the NBR — they are set by each municipality's building by-law. The stage list in section C is compiled from general practice and must be verified against the specific municipality's by-law.
- **[NA]** The specific mandatory inspection stages a Council-authorised agent calls in Windhoek, and in Okongo, were not verified.
- **[ZA]** Whether a plumbing certificate of compliance is a national requirement or a municipal one — not verified; Regulation A18 controls the trade of plumbing but the certificate requirement appears to be municipal.
- **DPC height above finished ground level** — the commonly-cited 150 mm was not verified against the current SANS 10400-K/J and is flagged accordingly.
- **[NA]** Whether Okongo Village Council has a published submission checklist or inspection regime — unverified.
- Current plan scrutiny **fee tariffs** for any specific authority in either country.

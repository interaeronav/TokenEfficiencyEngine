# Key SANS standards referenced on site — register by discipline

**Summary.** Beyond the SANS 10400 series, a working southern African project file touches perhaps thirty other SANS standards. This register groups them by discipline and gives, for each, the number, title, current edition where verifiable from the SABS webstore on 2026-08-25, and what the standard actually governs. Editions marked *not verified* did not return a result in the catalogue search performed — they may be current, superseded or withdrawn, and must be checked with SABS Standards Sales.

> ⚠️ Standard numbers and titles are stable and safe to quote. **Edition years, clause numbers and table values are not** — every value in a design must come from the purchased current edition.

## Structural loading and design

### SANS 10160 — Basis of structural design and actions for buildings and industrial structures

The eight-part loading code, and the single most important structural document in South African practice. It replaced SABS 0160 in 2010 and is Eurocode-aligned in structure (limit states, partial factors, action combinations) while retaining South African wind and seismic data.

- **Part 1 — Basis of structural design and actions.** Limit states, design situations, reliability differentiation, partial factors on actions, combination rules, and the framework the other parts plug into.
- **Part 2 — Self-weight and imposed loads.** Densities of materials, imposed floor loads by occupancy, roof imposed loads, barrier and balustrade line loads (cross-referenced by SANS 10400-N 4.4.4 for glass balustrades).
- **Part 3 — Wind actions.** The governing part for southern Africa. Fundamental basic wind speed map, terrain categories, topographic factors, pressure coefficients. Revised in 2019.
- **Part 4 — Seismic actions and general requirements for buildings.** Establishes where seismic design is required, based on the South African seismic hazard map. Also carries robustness/general requirements.
- **Part 5 — Basis for geotechnical design and actions.** Geotechnical categories, ground investigation requirements, earth pressures, and the geotechnical limit states.
- **Part 6 — Actions induced by cranes and machinery.**
- **Part 7 — Thermal actions.**
- **Part 8 — Actions during execution.** Temporary works, construction loads, formwork.

SANS 10400-B clause 4.2.1.2 requires representative permanent, imposed and seismic actions in accordance with the relevant part of SANS 10160, and clause 4.2.1.3 sets a floor of **0,37 kPa** design wind pressure for dwelling houses and **0,45 kPa** for elements.

### SANS 10100 — The structural use of concrete

- **Part 1 (2000, Ed. 2.02) — Design.** Limit-state design of reinforced and prestressed concrete. Still the 2000 edition; users must read it alongside SANS 10160 for actions.
- **Part 2 (2014, Ed. 3.00) — Materials and execution of work.** Concrete constituents, mix design, placing, curing, formwork, tolerances, testing.

### SANS 10162 — The structural use of steel

- **Part 1 (2011) — Limit-states design of hot-rolled steelwork.**
- **Part 2 (2011) — Limit-states design of cold-formed steelwork.** The relevant part for light-gauge steel framing and light steel frame building (LSFB) systems.
- **Part 4 (1997) — Cold-formed stainless steel structural members.**

### SANS 10163 — The structural use of timber

- **Part 1 (2003) — Limit-states design.**
- **Part 2 (2001) — Allowable stress design.** Still widely used by truss design software and for simple roof carpentry.

### SANS 10164 — The structural use of masonry

- **Part 1 (1980) — Unreinforced masonry walling.** Very old but never withdrawn; the design basis behind the SANS 10400-K wall panel tables.
- **Part 2 (2008) — Structural design and requirements for reinforced and prestressed masonry.**

## Concrete, cement and materials

- **SANS 50197-1** is the cement specification — the EN 197-1 adoption. It defines the CEM I to CEM V cement types, strength classes (32,5 / 42,5 / 52,5, with N and R rate-of-hardening designations) and conformity criteria. A bag of cement sold in South Africa carries the SANS 50197-1 designation and the SABS mark. **[NA]** Namibia has its own **Cement Regulations under the Standards Act 2005 (GN 259)** administered by the NSI — cement placed on the Namibian market is subject to that compulsory specification, not to the SABS mark alone.
- **SANS 1083** — aggregates from natural sources for concrete: grading envelopes, flakiness, fines content, deleterious materials, and the 10 % FACT / ACV strength tests.
- **SANS 878** — ready-mixed concrete. *Edition not verified; this number did not return in the SABS catalogue search and may have been superseded.* The commercial ready-mix market in South Africa is in practice governed by the Southern Africa Readymix Association (SARMA) audit scheme plus SANS 10100-2.
- **SANS 1200 series** — the old standardized specification for civil engineering construction (SANS 1200 A general, AB engineer's office, C site clearance, D earthworks, DB earthworks for pipe trenches, G concrete structural, GA concrete small works, L medium-pressure pipelines, LB bedding for pipes, LD sewers, ME subbase, MF base). *Not verified in the current catalogue.* It has largely been superseded by the **SANS 2001 construction works series** for building work, but the SANS 1200 references persist in older bills of quantities and in municipal standard specifications, so an agent reading a legacy BoQ will still encounter them.
- **SANS 2001 series — Construction works.** The modern replacement family. The parts most used on building sites: **CM1 masonry walling**, **CM2 strip footings, pad footings and slabs-on-the-ground**, **CT1/CT2 structural concrete**, **CC1 concrete works (structural)**, **DP series** for pipework. These are *specifications for execution*, written to be referenced directly from a contract, complementing the *design* codes above.
- **SANS 10145** — concrete masonry construction: workmanship code for laying concrete masonry.

## Masonry

- **SANS 227 — Burnt clay masonry units.** Classifies clay bricks by use (facing extra FBX, facing standard FBS, facing aggregate FBA, non-facing plastered NFP, non-facing extra NFX, engineering) and by compressive strength class. This is the standard a specification cites when it says "NFP clay brick to SANS 227, minimum nominal compressive strength 14 MPa".
- **SANS 1215 — Concrete masonry units.** Dimensions, tolerances, compressive strength classes, drying shrinkage and moisture movement for concrete bricks and blocks. Note the edition is still 2008.
- **SANS 50771-3 / -4** — the EN 771 adoptions for aggregate concrete masonry units and autoclaved aerated concrete masonry units.
- **SANS 2001-CM1 — Masonry walling.** The execution specification: setting out, mortar classes (class I, II, III), bedding, joint finishing, wall ties, damp-proof courses, control and articulation joints, bracing during construction, tolerances. This is what a mason is contractually held to.
- **SANS 10400-K** carries the deemed-to-satisfy wall panel geometry (see `02_sans-10400-parts-register.md`).

## Timber

- **SANS 1783-1 to -5** — the sawn softwood timber family. **Part 2 is the one that matters most**: stress-graded structural timber, giving the S5, S7 and S10 grades used throughout South African and Namibian roof carpentry. **Part 3** covers timber for roof trusses specifically; **Part 4** brandering and battens.
- **SANS 1288 — Preservative-treated timber.** Defines the hazard classes (H2 to H6) and required retentions for CCA, creosote and other preservatives. Cited normatively by SANS 10400-L. Structural roof timber in South Africa and Namibia is normally specified **H2 (interior, above ground, protected)** or **H3 (exterior, above ground)**; poles in ground contact are H4 or H5.
- **SANS 10163-1/-2** — the design codes (above).
- **SANS 10082 — Timber frame buildings.** *Edition not verified.* The reference document for platform-frame and balloon-frame timber construction, including bracing, sheathing and connection detailing; used together with SANS 10400-K 4.3 (timber-framed walls).
- **SANS 457-2 / 457-3** — wooden poles, droppers, guardrail posts and spacer blocks (softwood and hardwood species), referenced normatively by SANS 10400-L.
- **SANS 1460** — laminated timber (glulam), referenced by SANS 10400-L.

## Roofing and waterproofing

- **SANS 10400-L** — the deemed-to-satisfy part (roof coverings and waterproofing systems, drainage and waterproofing of flat roofs, timber roof construction, fire resistance and combustibility).
- **SANS 542 — Concrete roofing tiles.** *Edition not verified.*
- **SANS 10021 — Waterproofing of buildings.** *Edition not verified.* The code of practice for waterproofing systems: substrate preparation, falls to outlets, upstands and terminations, membrane types (torch-on bituminous, liquid-applied, single-ply), and detailing at penetrations and movement joints. The de facto reference for any flat-roof or podium waterproofing specification, and for waterproofing warranty disputes.
- **SANS 952-1 / -2 — Polyolefin film for damp-proofing and waterproofing in buildings.** The DPM under a ground-bearing slab and the DPC in a wall are specified to these.
- **SANS 248 — Bituminous damp-proof courses.**
- **SANS 10400-L 4.3** governs falls, outlets and overflow provisions on flat roofs.

## Water, drainage and sanitation

- **SANS 10252-1 — Water supply installations for buildings.** Pipe sizing, materials, isolation, backflow prevention, pressure control, hot water storage and distribution, and the hot water demand tables that SANS 10400-XA uses to compute the annual average hot water heating requirement.
- **SANS 10252-2 — Drainage installations for buildings.** Still the 1993 edition.
- **SANS 10400-P — Drainage** — the deemed-to-satisfy part: fixture provision, discharge pipe and drain sizing, traps, gullies, access, testing, and on-site treatment (conservancy tanks, septic tanks, french drains) with the percolation test method.
- **SANS 10400-Q — Non-water-borne sanitary disposal** — chemical toilets and ventilated improved pit (VIP) toilets. The relevant standard wherever there is no sewer.
- **SANS 10400-R — Stormwater disposal.**
- **SANS 10254 — Fixed electric storage water heating systems (installation, maintenance, replacement and repair).** *Edition not verified.* This is the geyser installation code: safety valves, vacuum breakers, drip trays and tray drains, expansion relief, tray and pipe insulation, and the position of the temperature-and-pressure safety valve. It is cross-referenced by SANS 10400-XA and by SANS 10142-1 for the electrical side, and by insurers for geyser burst claims.

## Electrical

- **SANS 10142-1 — The wiring of premises: low-voltage installations.** The wiring code. Current edition **2026 (Ed. 3.03)**. Governs everything from earthing and bonding, circuit protection and cable sizing to socket-outlet heights, earth leakage protection, and — critically for building control — the **Certificate of Compliance (CoC)** that a registered electrician must issue. **[ZA]** No occupancy certificate will issue under Act 103 of 1977 s 14 without the electrical CoC. **[NA]** Namibian local authorities and the regional electricity distributors require an equivalent electrical certificate before energising a connection; SANS 10142-1 is the reference wiring standard in practice.
- **SANS 10142-2 — Medium-voltage installations above 1 kV a.c. not exceeding 22 kV a.c.** Relevant where a project takes an MV supply and owns its own mini-substation — common on larger Namibian rural and lodge projects.

## Glazing

- **SANS 10137 — The installation of glazing in buildings.** Glass selection for wind load and human impact, edge cover, glazing systems, setting blocks, sealants, and sizing charts. Current edition **2026 (Ed. 4.01)**.
- **SANS 1263-1 — Safety and security glazing materials for buildings: safety performance of glazing materials under human impact.** Defines the impact classes for toughened and laminated safety glass. This is the standard named in SANS 10400-N 4.4.1 as the performance requirement for safety glazing, and the marking that must remain visible after installation.
- **SANS 613 — Fenestration products: mechanical performance criteria.** *Edition not verified.* Air infiltration, water penetration resistance, structural performance and operating force for windows and doors. Referenced by SANS 10400-N and by SANS 10400-XA for permissible air leakage of external vertical glazing.
- **SANS 727 — Windows and doors made from rolled mild steel sections.** Referenced by SANS 10400-N.

## Fire

- **SANS 10400-T / -W** — the deemed-to-satisfy parts (see `08_fire-safety-requirements.md`).
- **SANS 10287 — Automatic sprinkler installations for fire-fighting purposes.** Cited by SANS 10400-T 4.16.4(a): where a sprinkler system designed, installed, maintained and configured by competent persons as a **life-safety installation** in accordance with SANS 10287 is provided, the maximum travel distance may be increased from 45 m to **60 m**.
- **SANS 543 — Fire hose reels.** SANS 10400-T 4.34.2 requires hose reels to comply with SANS 543 and to be installed per SANS 10105-2 and SANS 10400-W.
- **SANS 1910 — Portable rechargeable fire extinguishers**; **SANS 1475-1/-2** — servicing of portable extinguishers and hose reels; **SANS 10105-1/-2** — the use and control of fire-fighting equipment. SANS 10400-T 4.37.3 requires extinguishers to comply with SANS 1910 and be installed, maintained and serviced by competent persons per SANS 1475-1 and SANS 10105-1.
- **SANS 10177 series** — fire tests on building materials, components and elements. SANS 10177-5 is the non-combustibility test cited in the SANS 10400-A definition of *non-combustible*.
- **SANS 11601 / SANS 1151** — transportable and mobile fire extinguishers.

## Energy and sustainability

- **SANS 10400-XA — Environmental sustainability: energy usage in buildings.** Mandatory in South Africa. Current edition 2026 (Ed. 3.00). See `06_energy-and-sustainability-requirements.md`.
- **SANS 204 — Energy efficiency in buildings.** Published as a **voluntary** national standard in October 2008 and updated in August 2010 to support SANS 10400-XA. The 2011 edition of XA referred out to SANS 204 for buildings exceeding the 15 % fenestration threshold, and for the R-value and shading calculation annexes (Table F.2 air spaces and air films, Table F.3 roof assemblies, clause 4.3.5.2 shading geometry). *Its current edition did not return in the SABS catalogue search on 2026-08-25* — the 2021 and 2026 editions of XA are more self-contained, and SANS 204 may have been withdrawn or restructured. **Verify with SABS before citing SANS 204 as current.**
- **SANS 10400-O** — natural lighting and ventilation thresholds interact directly with XA fenestration limits.

## Standards that regulate the professionals, not the building

- **SANS 10400-A Annex D Forms 1–4** — the declaration and certification forms.
- **Agrément South Africa certificates** — for innovative or non-standard building systems with no SANS standard. SANS 10400-B has a dedicated Agrément clause; SANS 10400-T 4.57.2 accepts Agrément-assessed safety distances for roofing systems in preference to the standard's own table. **[NA]** Namibia has no equivalent body; Agrément SA certificates are accepted informally as evidence of fitness for purpose.

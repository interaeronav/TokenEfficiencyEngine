# Paving manufacturers and suppliers in southern Africa and Namibia

**Summary.** The southern African paving industry is organised around the **Concrete Manufacturers Association of Southern Africa (CMA)**, now in its 45th year, which represents the precast concrete industry, publishes the standard technical manuals, initiates standards in cooperation with SABS, and runs a searchable producer-member directory. Below it sit a handful of large national producers (Technicrete, Bosun, Corobrik, Infraset), strong regional producers (Cape Brick, C.E.L., Inca, Vanstone, MVA, Deranco, Brickcast), and a specialist wet-cast segment (SmartStone, Revelstone). Namibian production is thinner and much harder to verify from outside the country; this file states clearly what is confirmed and what is not.

## The Concrete Manufacturers Association of Southern Africa

The CMA describes itself as "the primary representative of the precast concrete industry", currently in its 45th year. It promotes precast concrete, publishes industry guides, runs workshops and training, "initiates standards in close cooperation with SABS", and collaborates with members on new products. Product segments represented: bricks and blocks; roof tiles; floor slabs; pipes, culverts and manholes; paving; retaining walls.

Its practical value to a specifier:
- **Free technical publications** — Concrete Block Paving Books 1–5 plus a Facilitator's Guide, and technical notes on drainage, steep slopes, earthworks, efflorescence, permeable paving, township roads, maintenance and the compressive/tensile-splitting strength correlation. All at cma.org.za/publications/paving.
- **Lockpave and Permpave** design software downloads.
- **A producer-member directory** searchable by producer name, keyword, province or product tag, via an interactive map.
- Membership itself is a quality signal — CMA producer members that hold the SABS mark are flagged in the association's own lists.

## Bosun

Established 1993 (the site also cites "in business since 1995" for its paver line), a leading manufacturer of paving, kerbs and retaining wall blocks from **advanced factories in Midrand (Gauteng), Brits (North West) and Port Elizabeth (Eastern Cape)**. Member of the Concrete Masonry & Hardscape Association (CMHA); associates listed include SmartStone, Cretesol, HESS Group, PPC, ICPI and NCMA.

Ranges: Designer Paving, Regular Paving, Interlocking Paving, Flagstone Paving, Permeable Paving, Tactile Pavers, Kerbs, Retaining Wall Blocks, Erosion Control (Buffalo Block), Streetscaping. Over **20 colours and textures**. A **five-year guarantee** is offered.

Verified product detail:
- **60 mm Interlocking Paver** — Type S-A, for commercial applications; grey in Premium (smooth) and Standard (rougher, cheaper) finishes at all branches; blended and flat colours; exposed-aggregate "Organic" finishes and "Colourfast" finishes at Midrand only; split-textured "Rio Rocoso" at Midrand and PE.
- **80 mm Interlocking Paver** — Type S-A, for heavy commercial and industrial; thicker paver also creates more friction and better interlock.
- **Bevel Bond Paver** — the cost-effective rectangular workhorse, **50 mm standard (60 mm can be manufactured)**, dual-layer (abrasion-resistant topping over a strength-focused base), bevelled edge to reduce chipping during installation.
- Other named products: Smooth Ethnic Paver, Split Bevel Bond, Rio Rocoso Split Bevel, Shale Interlocking Paver, Rio Rocoso Interlocker, Citylock 60 mm and 80 mm, Organic Interlocking Paver, Split Interlocking Paver.
- All three factories were **among the first to be awarded the SANS 1058:2021 mark**, and each branch has a fully equipped testing laboratory.

## Corobrik

South Africa's largest clay brick maker, and a significant concrete producer. Divisions: clay face bricks (including a Slimline range and Breezeblock), **clay paving**, concrete paving, concrete bricks and blocks, Geolock earth retaining system, and Terraforce retaining blocks. Provides a face brick calculator, BIM libraries, brochures, technical guides, a visualizer, and a bricklayer certification programme.

Clay paving is classified to **SANS 1575** (classes PA and PB — see `02_southern-african-design-standards.md`). Shape families: **Modular** (rectangular), **Cobble** (square), **Doppio**, **Piazza** (narrow plank). Thicknesses **50, 65 and 73 mm**. Colour ranges include Burgundy, Cederberg, Champagne, De Hoop (Matt Brown, Red, Strand, Multiblend), Graphite, Ironstone, Namaquastone, Nougat, Nutmeg, Onyx and Tuscan Blend.

## Infraset

Manufactures roof tiles (Sunset, Horizon, Sunrise 4.4 profiles in six through-colours plus a multicolour "vintage" finish), Eco Shield radiant barrier insulation, **paving and cobbles**, retaining walls (from 1 m to 15 m walls, angles 60°–90°), erosion control products, kerbs, and embankment seating and steps. Paving is offered in "numerous shapes, sizes, finishes and strengths, in virtually any colour or multi-colour variant", produced with computerised concrete batching to SANS requirements under an ISO 9001 quality management system. Contact points in Durban and Johannesburg.

> ⚠️ Note the domain trap: **infraset.co.za** is the South African precast manufacturer. **infraset.com** is an unrelated site (a template page for a Berlin bakery) — do not cite it.

## Cape Brick

Cape Town concrete masonry manufacturer based in **Philippi**, selling direct to the public, to building contractors and to resellers across the Western Cape (South Peninsula, Strand, Stellenbosch, Northern Suburbs). Product range: bricks, maxis, dumpies, blocks, coloured blocks, U-blocks, retainers, steps and seating. Emphasises **eco-friendly / recycled-content** concrete masonry and publishes technical material on recommended mixes, quantities, control joints and brickforce, lime in mortar, and recycling. Contact: 021 690 2020, sales@capebrick.co.za.

## C.E.L. Paving Products

Western Cape concrete paver manufacturer, +27 21 905 5998, info@celpaving.co.za, a CMA member since at least 2004. Three product streams: **Residential** (pool surrounds, walkways, driveways, steps, patios, water-wise applications), **Commercial** (public spaces, car parks, shopping centres, residential estates, paved roads, permeable paving) and **Civils and Industrial** (roads, kerbing, traffic circles, factories, specialised heavy-duty pavers, permeable paving). Publishes downloadable brochures.

## SmartStone and Revelstone — the wet-cast segment

These two are worth distinguishing from the block makers because their product is **wet-cast cast stone**, not dry-cast block: a flowable concrete cast into moulds that reproduce stone texture, giving a much wider range of formats, surfaces and colours, but a different structural product (large slabs, low interlock, generally laid on mortar or on sand for pedestrian use).

- **SmartStone** — founded 1980; branches throughout southern Africa, each with its own custom-built factory and show area; some branches offer landscape design and accredited installation. Ranges: cobbles, flagstones, copings and edging, concrete tiles, cladding, landscaping products, large concrete slabs, streetscaping products, walling. A member of the American **ICPI** (Interlocking Concrete Pavement Institute) and **NCMA**, and publishes ICPI technical specifications for the local market — a genuinely useful free resource, and unusual in that it explicitly documents "what can go wrong".
- **Revelstone** — "manufacturers and developers of cast stone products since 1993", Cape Town area with nationwide delivery. Ranges include Kent, Jura, Ravine and Klompie collections: pavers (straight edge and random edge), tiles, cobbles in **50 mm and 75 mm**, steps, kerbs, cladding, Devon capping, coping, fire pits, and klompies in 15 mm, 30 mm and 75 mm. **16 versatile colours** plus custom shades.

## Namibian producers and suppliers **[NA]**

**What is verified:**
- **Brick & Concrete Industries (Namibia)** appears in the CMA's own 2004 paving producer-member list with a Windhoek telephone number, **+264 61 280 4103**. This is the only Namibian paving producer that could be confirmed from a primary source.
- **Ohorongo Cement** is the Namibian cement producer: established 2007 by Schwenk Zement KG (Germany) as an autonomous Namibian-registered company; Sargberg plant north of Otavi; head office in Klein Windhoek; an N$2,5 billion investment built over 22 months; runs the Ohorongo Otavi Community Trust. Cement types and capacity unverified.
- **Roads Contractor Company (RCC)** is the state-owned road contractor with a head office on Marien Ngouabi Street, Windhoek, regional offices in Otjiwarongo and Keetmanshoop, 8 satellite locations, and units for grading, re-gravelling, bitumen, earth dams, low-volume seal roads and railway embankments. It is a contractor, not a paver manufacturer, but it is the largest holder of road plant in the country.

**What could not be verified:** no paver or block manufacturer in **Windhoek, Oshakati, Ondangwa or Tsumeb** could be confirmed from an accessible primary source. Search engines were unavailable during compilation (blocked or bot-challenged), the Namibian Yellow Pages directory has no paving or brick category exposed, and candidate `.com.na` domains for paving companies did not resolve.

**How to close this gap (recommended actions, in order):**
1. Contact the CMA (cma.org.za) and ask its member directory for producers in Namibia — the association explicitly covers "Southern Africa" and historically had Namibian, Botswanan and Kenyan members.
2. Telephone Brick & Concrete Industries in Windhoek on the 2004 number and ask who manufactures pavers in the northern regions.
3. Contact the major Namibian builders' merchants — Pupkewitz Megabuild, Build It Namibia, Woermann Brock and Cymot are the recognised chains — and ask which local plants they buy from in Oshakati/Ondangwa. `needs-verification`.
4. Ask Ohorongo Cement's technical/marketing department: cement producers know every block maker in their catchment.
5. Ask the Roads Authority regional office and the Ohangwena Regional Council which suppliers are on their approved lists.
6. Consider the manufacture option directly (`05_paving-manufacture.md`) — at 200 km from Ondangwa and 700 km from Windhoek, a local plant may beat any supplier.
